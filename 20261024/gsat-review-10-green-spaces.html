一句話總評：兩段分工正確，第二段也用 First／Second／Third 列出三個措施，架構清楚；但選的是「缺乏綠地」，內容卻一路寫成空氣污染和工廠污水，三個措施有兩個在講汽車排碳，和綠地無關，句構錯誤也很密集。

扣分主因排序：① 主題偏移：選「綠地不足」，卻寫成「環境污染」，措施 1、2 都在管汽車排放。② 第一段沒說明「為什麼最想解決這個」，缺少綠地對生活品質的影響。③ 一句兩個動詞、are must be、only everyone work… can we 這類句構錯誤。

02

題目

題本原文

每個城市都有其獨特的問題，改善這些問題可以提升市民的生活品質。如果你能選擇解決其中一個問題，你會選擇哪一個？

  1. 第一段說明你最想解決哪一個問題，並解釋原因。
  2. 第二段說明你希望有關當局採取哪些具體措施來解決這個問題。
PICTURE 1Public Transportation Issues：車廂擁擠
PICTURE 2Lack of Green Spaces：高樓林立、綠地很少
PICTURE 3Crime and Safety：警察壓制嫌犯

這次偏掉的：措施要解決的是「綠地不足」，不是「空氣污染」。

04

詳細錯誤訂正

答案目前蓋住中。先對照每一條的錯誤代碼自己改寫，再按上方按鈕掀開。

PARAGRAPH 1

01I will choose the second one.WWT
➡️I would most like to solve the second problem, the lack of green spaces.

題目是假設語氣「如果你能選擇」，用 would 比 will 自然。the second one 太模糊，要直接寫出問題名稱，閱卷老師一看就知道你選什麼。

02The picture illustrate the phenomenon in modern society.SV
➡️The picture illustrates a common problem in modern cities.

The picture 是第三人稱單數，動詞要加 s。the phenomenon 沒說是什麼現象，直接說 a common problem 更清楚。

03With the rapid of industry advanceCH
➡️With the rapid development of industry

rapid 是形容詞，後面要接名詞。「工業的快速發展」英文語序是 the rapid development of industry。

04the cities become more convenience than beforeWWT
➡️cities have become more convenient than before

become 後面接形容詞 convenient，convenience 是名詞。「從以前到現在變得更方便」用現在完成式 have become。

05But it cause a serious problem to the environment.SVWW
➡️However, this has caused serious damage to the environment.

it 是單數，動詞要用 causes，或改成現在完成式 has caused。But 不放句首，用 However。cause damage to 是固定搭配。

06Governments build the houses quickly and cutting down the trees, the factories produce polluted water in the lake.ROCH
➡️Buildings go up quickly while trees are cut down, and factories release polluted water into lakes.

build 和 cutting 不對等，要都用同一種形式。兩個完整句子只用逗號連接是逗號錯誤，要加 and。蓋房子的多半是建商，不是政府，主詞改成 Buildings 比較準確。

07In short time maybe we can't discuss it, but in long term may be a big effect.PREPMISSWW
➡️We may not notice the problem in the short term, but in the long run, it may have a huge effect.

固定說法是 in the short term／in the long run，都要加 the。discuss 是「討論」，這裡要表達的是「察覺不到」，用 notice。後半句少了主詞 it，而且「造成影響」要說 have an effect。

PARAGRAPH 2

08lack of gree spaces is a serious effect in futureSPARTWW
➡️the lack of green spaces will become a serious problem in the future

green 拼錯。the lack of 和 in the future 都要加 the。「缺乏綠地」本身是問題，不是影響，所以用 problem 不用 effect。

09Cars release carbon dioxide frequently and turn the parks into a parking lot, these two phenomenon are must be changed.ROPLCHLOG
➡️Many parks have been turned into parking lots for the growing number of cars. This must be changed.

兩個完整句子用逗號連接是逗號錯誤。phenomenon 的複數是 phenomena。are must be 是兩個動詞疊在一起，只要 must be。另外「汽車排碳」是空氣污染，不是綠地問題；「公園變停車場」才和綠地直接有關，留這一個就好。

10I have some advices for the government.PL
➡️I have some suggestions for the government.

advice 是不可數名詞，不加 s。要用可數的話改成 suggestions。

11First, request every who drive the car must to check the device releasing the carbon dioxide under the policy.CONTCH
➡️First, the government could turn unused parking lots and empty land into small parks.

這個措施和綠地無關，要換掉。文法上也有問題：every 後面不能直接接 who，要說 everyone who 或 all drivers；must 後面接原形動詞，不加 to。如果真的要寫排氣檢查，正確說法是 require all drivers to have their cars' emissions checked。

12Second, donate citizen who don't have enough budget to replace the old car.CONTWWPL
➡️Second, new buildings should be required to include rooftop gardens or green areas.

這個措施也和綠地無關。文法上：donate 是「捐東西」，補助人要用 offer subsidies to；citizen 要用複數；「沒錢」說 cannot afford to。正確說法是 offer subsidies to citizens who cannot afford to replace their old cars。

13grow more trees and plants on the sidewalksWW
➡️plant more trees along the sidewalks

grow 是讓植物長大，「種樹」更常用 plant。沿著人行道用 along 比 on 自然。這個措施方向正確，要保留。

14the air more cleanerWW
➡️the air cleaner

cleaner 已經是比較級，前面不能再加 more。

15Last but not least, only everyone work hard together can we have a better life in the future.WWSVCH
➡️In conclusion, only if everyone works together can we enjoy a better life in the future.

Last but not least 是用來帶出「最後一點」，不能當結論的開頭，結論用 In conclusion 或 All in all。only 倒裝句的前半要是完整子句：only if + 主詞 + 動詞，everyone 是單數，動詞用 works。

05

高分優化建議

切題度：全篇都要扣緊「綠地」

  • 選了 Lack of Green Spaces，第一段卻在寫工業發展、工廠排放污水，第二段又寫汽車排碳。這些是「環境污染」，不是「綠地不足」。閱卷老師會判定主題偏移。
  • 第一段的原因要說「缺乏綠地對生活的影響」，例如：沒地方散步運動、小孩沒地方玩、夏天城市太熱、壓力大沒地方放鬆。
  • 第二段的措施都要能「增加綠地」：把閒置空地或停車場改成小公園、規定新大樓要有屋頂花園、沿人行道種樹。
  • 寫完每個措施，問自己一句：「這樣做，綠地會變多嗎？」答案是否定的就換掉。

組織邏輯：第一句就寫出選擇和主題

  • 開頭直接寫 Of the three problems, I would most like to solve the lack of green spaces.，主題一開始就定下來，後面比較不會寫偏。
  • 第一段最後可以加一句收尾，回扣題目的「生活品質」：Without green spaces, city life becomes stressful and unhealthy.
  • 結論不要用 Last but not least。In conclusion 後面接一句說明這些措施會帶來什麼好處。

06

滿分示範改寫

保留原本的想法：選第二張圖、城市發展快、高樓多、樹被砍掉、公園變停車場、人行道種樹、讓人更健康空氣更乾淨、大家一起努力。換掉的是兩個和綠地無關的汽車措施。

Of the three problems, I would most like to solve the lack of green spaces. With the rapid development of industry, cities have become more convenient than ever before. However, this convenience has come at a price. High-rise buildings go up quickly while trees are cut down, and many parks have even been turned into parking lots. As a result, city residents have fewer places to take a walk, exercise, or simply relax after a long day. Children have no safe place to play, and the city becomes hotter every summer. We may not notice the problem in the short term, but in the long run, it will seriously affect our quality of life.

To solve this problem, I have three suggestions for the government. First, it could turn unused parking lots and empty land into small neighborhood parks. Second, new buildings should be required to include rooftop gardens or green areas. Third, more trees should be planted along the sidewalks, so that people can enjoy shade and fresh air on their way to school or work. These measures would not only make the air cleaner but also make citizens healthier and happier. In conclusion, only if the government and citizens work together can we turn our concrete jungle into a greener and more livable city.
