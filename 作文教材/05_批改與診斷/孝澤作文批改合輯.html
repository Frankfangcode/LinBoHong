建議

內容豐富、架構清楚、有邏輯連貫性。有理解題目要求，第一段比較兩個公園，第二段闡述「理想中的公園」。符合學測要求。

待改進處：

  • 文法錯誤較多，且集中在動詞形式與冠詞 (a/an/the) 使用。
  • 句型變化不足，多為並列句或重複結構。
  • 搭配詞 (collocation) 可以更自然。
  • 字彙可以再提升層次。

作文逐句詳解

原文 (Original) 修正與建議 (Corrections & Suggestions)
There are variety of park, and it may presents in different appearance or feature. There are is a variety of parks, and it they may presents in different appearances or features. [文法] "a variety of + 複數名詞" 是單數主詞 (is)；may 後面要接原形動詞 (present)。
Picture A shows a entertaining park for the children. Picture A shows an entertaining park for children. [文法] entertaining 是母音開頭，用 an；children 泛指兒童，不需加 the。
The government place numerous facilities for kids to do their favorite pastime. The government places numerous facilities for kids to enjoy their favorite pastimes. [文法] 主詞 The government 是單數，動詞要加 s。[用字]「從事」休閒活動用 enjoy 比 do 更貼切。
...there are a lot of vendors, rolling hotdogs... ...there are a lot of vendors, selling hotdogs... [用字] 攤販是「販售」(selling) 熱狗，不是熱狗自己滾 (rolling)。
...broaden their relationship of people, which can help them to grow up. ...broaden their social circles, which can help them grow. [用字]「拓展人際關係」更自然的說法是 broaden their social circles；help them (to) grow 都可以。
Picture B shows a tranquil park, the trees are planted everywhere, which can let people get close to nature. Picture B shows a tranquil park. The trees planted everywhere allow people to get close to nature. [句型] 用分詞構句 (planted everywhere) 當形容詞，讓句子更簡潔有力。
In addition, birds chisped cheerfully... In addition, birds chirped cheerfully... [拼字] (鳥叫) 是 chirp。
My idea park is that it can adapt to all kinds of people. My ideal park is one that can cater to all kinds of people. [用字]「理想的」公園是 ideal；「迎合...的需求」用 cater to 比 adapt (適應) 更精準。
There are divided into two parts. It is divided into two parts. [文法] 句子需要主詞 (It)，指代的是「公園」。
...the other one is for the people who want to relax and enjoy the nature. ...the other part is for people who want to unwind and immerse themselves in nature. [用字]「放鬆並沉浸在...」 是更生動的表達。
...a fabulous park need to consider anyone's feeling and need. ...a fabulous park needs to consider anyone's feelings and needs. [文法] 主詞 a park 是單數，動詞 (needs) 要加 s。feelings 和 needs 在此是可數名詞。
...to make their childhood perfect. ...to create memorable childhood memories. [用字] 讓童年「完美」(perfect) 太絕對，改成「創造難忘的童年回憶」更佳。
...they must need to a space to relieve the stress. ...they must need to definitely need a space to relieve the their stress. [文法] must 和 need to 語意重複；紓解「他們的」壓力，用 their stress。
They can sit by the tree or under the tree. They can sit under the shade of a tree. [用字]「坐在樹蔭下」 是更道地的說法。
...listen to sound coming from the nature. ...listen to the sounds coming from nature. [文法] 來自大自然的「各種聲音」是可數的 (sounds)；泛指「大自然」時，nature 前不加 the。
All in all, my idea park is suitable to any people. All in all, my ideal park is suitable for all people. [用字] 介系詞搭配：suitable for...
I hope that If I erect this park one day... I hope that if this park is built one day... [用字] erect (豎立) 多用於紀念碑，蓋公園用 build 即可。[文法] 句中的 if 不需大寫。
...everyone's life can be better. ...everyone's life will be better. [文法] 這是「未來」的假設，使用 will be (未來式) 更精確。

重點學習：好用句型與替換詞

高分搭配詞 (Collocations)

  • Take into account (考量到...)
  • expand one's social circle (拓展社交圈)
  • relieve one's stress (紓解壓力)
  • get close to nature (親近自然)
  • sit under the shade of a tree (坐在樹蔭下)

進階句型 (Advanced Patterns)

1. It is important that + S + (should) + V...

例句：It is important that a park (should) consider everyone's needs.

2. S + V, so that + S + can + V...

例句：Trees are planted everywhere so that people can enjoy the fresh air.

3. On one hand, ... On the other hand, ...

例句：On one hand, kids can play; on the other hand, adults can rest.

4. Not only... but also... (倒裝句)

例句：Not only does the park offer fun, but it also provides peace and relaxation.

詞彙升級 (Vocabulary Upgrade)

基本詞 可替換 例句
good beneficial / favorable / pleasant A park provides a beneficial environment for both mind and body.
important essential / significant / crucial It is crucial to design spaces that serve everyone's needs.
happy delighted / joyful / content Children feel joyful when playing with friends in the park.
beautiful picturesque / stunning / appealing The picturesque scenery makes visitors linger.
people visitors / citizens / residents The park attracts many visitors from nearby communities.
help enable / allow / contribute to Trees in the park contribute to a cleaner environment.

搭配詞 中文意思 例句

take a stroll 散步 Elderly people enjoy taking a stroll along the path.
