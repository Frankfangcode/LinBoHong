詳細錯誤訂正

答案目前蓋住中。先對照每一條的錯誤代碼自己改寫，再按上方按鈕掀開。

PARAGRAPH 1

01As a proud citizen of Taiwan, there are two things make me feel very proud of my country.CHREF
➡️As a citizen of Taiwan, I can name two things that make me proud of my country:

there are 和 make 是兩個動詞疊在一起，中間要加 that。另外，As a citizen of Taiwan 描述的是「我」，所以主要子句的主詞要是 I，不能是 there。proud 前後出現兩次，刪掉一個。

02Our friendly people and our delicious food.FRAGP
➡️…my country: our friendly people and our delicious food.

這句只有名詞、沒有動詞，是不完整句。把前一句的句號改成冒號，直接接在後面列出兩件事。

03so many yummy choicesWW
➡️countless mouthwatering choices

yummy 是口語、偏兒語，作文用 delicious 或 mouthwatering。

04at very cheap pricesWW
➡️at very affordable prices

東西可以 cheap，但「價格」要說 low 或 affordable，不說 cheap prices。

PARAGRAPH 2

05To let more people know more about the feature of Taiwan. We can use…FRAGPL
➡️To help more people learn about these features of Taiwan, we can use…

To 開頭的目的片語不能單獨成句，要用逗號接主要子句。第一段講了兩個特色，feature 要用複數，加上 these 指回第一段。

06short vidoSPPL
➡️short videos

video 拼錯。泛指影片要用複數。

07to introduce our cityWW
➡️to introduce our country

題目講的是臺灣，是一個國家，不是一個城市。

08we can use Youtube and Instragram the show foreign how was our cultureSPWWCH
➡️we can use YouTube and Instagram to show foreigners what our culture is like

這句錯最多。YouTube、Instagram 是品牌名，拼法和大小寫要照原樣。the 應該是 to（用來做某事）。foreign 是形容詞，「外國人」是 foreigners。how was our culture 是問句語序，放在句子中間要改成直述語序；「文化是什麼樣子」英文說 what our culture is like。

09Thease videos ／ the governtment ／ Whe they shareSP
➡️These videos ／ the government ／ When they share

三個都是會寫的字，是寫太快漏掉或多寫字母。第二段一共 5 個拼字錯誤，交卷前留 2 分鐘從頭掃一次就能抓到。

10will want to visit to TaiwanPREP
➡️will want to visit Taiwan

visit 是及物動詞，後面直接接地點，不加 to。比較：go to Taiwan、travel to Taiwan、visit Taiwan。

11the world will understand the love and feature in TaiwanREFPL
➡️the world will get to know the warmth of our people and the flavors of our food

the love and feature 太模糊，讀者不知道指什麼。結尾直接點名第一段的兩個特色（人和食物），整篇就首尾呼應了。

Q1 ｜ 預估 2 / 4

自 2007 年營運以來，高鐵（the High Speed Rail）已成為臺灣最便利、最快速的交通工具之一。

From 2007 ago, the high speed rail has become one of the most convenience, fastest transpation in Taiwan.

➡️Since it began operating in 2007, the High Speed Rail has become one of the most convenient and fastest means of transportation in Taiwan.

WW　ago 前面要接一段時間（two years ago），不能接年份。has become 是現在完成式，要搭配 Since 2007。

MISS　漏譯「營運」：Since it began operating in 2007。

WW　convenience 是名詞，修飾交通工具要用形容詞 convenient。這是詞性錯誤，上週翻譯也出現過。

PL / SP　one of the 後面要接複數名詞。「交通工具」是 means of transportation（means 單複數同形），transportation 也拼錯了。兩個最高級之間要加 and。

高分優化建議

切題度：第二段要扣回第一段的兩個特色

  • 題目第二段問的是怎麼介紹「這些臺灣特色」，也就是第一段寫的人和食物。目前第二段寫的是「我們的文化」「我們的城市」，方法不錯，但沒有說要拍什麼。
  • 直接把兩個特色放進方法裡：拍一支「臺灣人幫迷路遊客指路」的短片；邀請外國 YouTuber 來做夜市美食之旅。這樣每個方法都對到第一段的一個特色。

  • 第二段：主題句 → First 社群影片 → Also 邀請 YouTuber → In these ways 收尾。也很清楚，只要把內容扣回兩個特色。

滿分示範改寫

內容都是他原本的：友善的人、夜市美食、社群影片、邀請外國 YouTuber。綠底是讓第二段扣回第一段的改動。

As a citizen of Taiwan, I can name two things that make me proud of my country: our friendly people and our delicious food. First, people in Taiwan are extremely kind and helpful. When foreigners get lost on the street, Taiwanese people are always happy to help them with a big smile. This warmth makes Taiwan a great place to live in and to visit. Second, Taiwan is famous for its amazing food, especially at night markets. From bubble milk tea to beef noodles, there are countless mouthwatering choices at very affordable prices. I feel proud whenever foreign visitors praise our food.

To help more people learn about these features of Taiwan, we can make good use of short videos and social media. First, we can post videos on YouTube and Instagram that show foreigners what everyday life in Taiwan is like, such as a local helping a lost tourist or a food tour of a busy night market. These videos can easily reach young people in different countries. Also, the government can invite famous foreign YouTubers to travel around Taiwan and taste our street food. When they share their experiences online, their fans will want to visit Taiwan, too. In these ways, the world will get to know the warmth of our people and the flavors of our food.
