Chapter 13 · 代名詞

代名詞重點整理

代名詞的唯一目的：代替前面講過的名詞，讓句子不重複囉唆。這份筆記把整章拆成 7 個重點，每個都附上例句、公式、和最容易考錯的陷阱。

Romeo texted a message to Juliet. → He texted a message to her.
Romeo loved Juliet. → He loved her.
1
Point 01

人稱代名詞

三個格位

人稱主格所有格受格
第一人稱單/複I / wemy / ourme / us
第二人稱youyouryou
第三人稱單he / she / ithis / her / itshim / her / it
第三人稱複theytheirthem

主格：當主詞

John is my brother. He is a college student.
John 是我哥哥。他是個大學生。

受格：及物動詞的受詞 / 介系詞的受詞

The teacher punished him for his mischief.
老師處罰他調皮搗蛋。
Her mother took good care of her.
她媽媽無微不至地照顧她。（介系詞 of 的受詞）

所有格：功能相當於形容詞，後面一定接名詞

I have a dog. Its name is Snoopy.
我養了一隻狗。牠的名字是 Snoopy。
★易混淆its＝所有格（它的，後接名詞）；it's＝ it is / it has 的縮寫。看後面接不接名詞就能分辨。
2
Point 02

it 的特殊用法

① 代替前面提過的特定名詞

My dad bought me a new cell phone, but I lost it the next day.
我爸買了新手機給我，但我隔天就弄丟了。

② 表時間／天氣／距離／日期（無實際意義的虛主詞）

時間It's ten past twelve.
天氣It is hot in summer.
距離It is 3 miles to the airport.

③ 強調句（分裂句）

It is/was + 強調部分 + that + S + V（剩下的句子）

原句：Amy ran into Jasmine in the restaurant yesterday.

It was Amy that ran into Jasmine in the restaurant yesterday.
強調主詞
It was Jasmine that Amy ran into in the restaurant yesterday.
強調受詞
It was in the restaurant that Amy ran into Jasmine yesterday.
強調地點
It was yesterday that Amy ran into Jasmine in the restaurant.
強調時間

檢查法：拿掉 It is/was 和 that，句子要能還原成完整句

✗It was the scary movie that I couldn't fall asleep.（拿掉後不通）
✓It was because of the scary movie that I couldn't fall asleep.

④ 虛主詞 / 虛受詞

因為真正的主詞／受詞太長，先用 it 佔位，避免頭重腳輕。

虛主詞

It + is/was + adj./N + { to VR ｜ V-ing ｜ that+S+V ｜ wh-+S+V }
It is thrilling to take a roller coaster ride.
坐雲霄飛車很刺激。
It is a known fact that Santa Claus doesn't really exist.
聖誕老人不存在是眾所皆知的事實。

虛受詞

主詞 + believe/think/find/make... + it + adj./N + { to VR ｜ that+S+V }
I find it hard to believe that J.K. Rowling used to live on welfare.
我很難相信 J.K. Rowling 曾經靠社會福利過活。

常用固定片語

make it clear把⋯表明清楚
make it possible (for sb.)使⋯能夠
make it a rule習慣做⋯
take it for granted視為理所當然

for sb. vs of sb. — 選詞關鍵題

It is + adj. + for sb. + to VR

形容詞修飾「這件事」本身：easy, important, dangerous, possible, necessary...

It is important for us to hand in homework on time.
It is + adj. + of sb. + to VR

形容詞形容「人的個性」：foolish, brave, kind, generous, rude...

It was brave of you to stand up to those bullies.
技巧把動作套進「他很＿＿」，說得通就用 of（他很勇敢 ✓）；說不通就用 for（不是「我們很重要」）。
3
Point 03

反身代名詞

單數myselfyourselfhimself / herself / itself
複數ourselvesyourselvesthemselves

常見拼字錯誤：ourselves / themselves 不能漏掉字尾的 s。

用法① 主詞與受詞是同一人 → 受詞用反身代名詞

She killed herself. He felt ashamed of himself.

用法② 加強語氣「某人自己⋯」

I myself do not like this idea.
我個人本身不喜歡這個點子。

用法③ (by) oneself ＝靠自己／獨自一人

I accomplished the task myself.（沒人幫忙）　I went to the wedding by myself.（獨自沒人陪）

用法④ 及物動詞 + 反身代名詞 ＝ be + p.p.（固定搭配，常考）

Mother Teresa devoted herself to helping the poor. ＝ was devoted to helping the poor.
✗不要漏掉 herself／themselves 等反身代名詞，且 to 是介系詞，後面接 V-ing。

反身代名詞慣用語（整組背）

by oneself獨自；靠自己
in itself本身；本質上
pride oneself on以⋯為榮
enjoy oneself玩得愉快
come to oneself甦醒
help oneself to自行取用
adapt oneself to習慣於
make yourself at home不必拘束
seat oneself坐下
absorb/lose oneself in專注於
commit oneself to下定決心做⋯
apply oneself to專心於
注意relax、rest、feel、concentrate 這類動詞後面接反身代名詞時要特別小心，很多情況下單獨使用（不加反身代名詞）才是正確的。
4
Point 04

所有代名詞

所有代名詞 ＝ 所有格 + 名詞
所有格myyourhisheritsourtheir
所有代名詞mineyourshishersitsourstheirs
I've got my ticket, but Cindy can't find hers.（= her ticket）

常考句型：Some/Any friend(s) of + 所有代名詞

Some friends of my dad's will visit him this Saturday.
＝ Some of my dad's friends will visit him.
✗不可直接照中文語序寫成 "my dad's some friends"。
5
Point 05

指示代名詞

this/these（近）vs that/those（遠）

This is my book; that is yours.

★ that / those 代替前面的名詞，避免重複（比較句必考）

單數名詞 → that　｜　複數名詞 → those　｜　絕不能用 this/these

The pace of life in a big city is faster than that（= the pace of life）in the country.
✗His educational background is different from his colleagues.
✓His educational background is different from that of his colleagues.（背景比背景，不是人比人）

前者⋯後者⋯

單數：that（前者）...this（後者）　｜　單複數皆可：the former...the latter（考試較常用）

She chose the former（family）instead of the latter（career）.

「凡是⋯的人」句型

先行詞關代動詞
those / people / theywho複數動詞
he / one / anyonewho單數動詞
I look down on those who are cruel to animals.
注意此句型的關係代名詞只能用 who，不能用 that。
6
Point 06

不定代名詞

both / all・either / any・neither / none

詞用於意思動詞
both兩者兩者皆複數
all三者以上全都依名詞而定
either兩者之一任一單數
any任何任何依名詞而定
neither兩者皆不單（正式）
none三者以上都不單（正式）
Both of your parents are great.（複數動詞）
A: Tea or coffee? B: Either is fine.（單數動詞）

some vs any

some → 肯定句、疑問句表邀請　｜　any → 疑問句、否定句、條件句

We need some flour. There isn't any left.

one / another / the other 家族 — 數字決定用字

one … the other總數＝2
one … another … the other總數＝3
one … another … the others總數＞3，其餘全部
I have two brothers. One is a teacher, and the other is a doctor.
There are 3 channels. One plays Titanic, another plays Shrek, and the other plays Avatar.
There are 4 students. One is studying. Another is on the computer. The others are chatting.
情境句型
還沒講完，任一個one ... another ...（可一直延伸）
只列舉兩三種，還有其他沒講some ... others ...
只有兩種類別，第二種＝全部剩下some ... the others ...
口訣還沒講完用 another / others（不加 the）；講到「最後剩下、確定的」就要加 the。

each vs every

each：強調「每一個分子」，可用於兩者以上，可單獨當代名詞。

Each of us has a locker.

every：強調「整體」，用於三者以上，不可單獨當代名詞。

✗ Every of us... → ✓ Every one of us has a locker.

each other（兩者互相）vs one another（三者以上互相）

George and Mary love each other. Bunny, Bear, and Piggy visit one another's houses.

Not + all/every/both = 部分否定

Not every student pays attention in class.
並非每個學生都認真聽（不是全部都不專心）。
7
Point 07

疑問代名詞

主格所有格受格
誰whowhosewhom
什麼what—what
哪一個which—which
Who is that man?（詢問人：姓名／身分／關係）
Whom would you like to invite?（受詞）　Whom did you talk to?（介系詞受詞）
注意口語中 whom 常被 who 取代，但正式書寫仍建議用 whom。
Whose book is this?
What are you talking about?（問東西／事）
Which do you like better, pork or beef?（特定範圍中做選擇）

最容易考錯的 7 個地方

如果時間不夠，優先複習這幾項

① it / that / one 選錯it＝同一個東西；that/those＝同類但不同一個（比較句必用）；one/ones＝同類、不特定的一個。
② for vs of形容「事」用 for，形容「人的個性」用 of。套「他很＿＿」通不通來判斷。
③ one/another/the other 數字題還沒講完用 another／others（不加 the）；最後剩下、確定的要加 the。
④ each 可當代名詞，every 不行Each of us ✓　／　Every of us ✗，要說 Every one of us。
⑤ 強調句拿掉 It...that 要能還原還原不成完整句，代表漏了介系詞（because of / in / while...）。
⑥ that/those 代替前面名詞中文常省略重複的名詞，但英文比較句一定要補回 that/those。
⑦ Not + all/every/both＝部分否定「不是全部」，不是「全部都不」。
