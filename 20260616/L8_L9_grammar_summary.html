L8-L9 英文文法重點整理：only倒裝句、倍數表達、助動詞推測

核心規則：什麼時候要倒裝？

Only 引導副詞修飾語（介系詞片語 / 副詞子句 / 時間副詞）放句首時，主句要倒裝。
只是主詞或受詞，❌ 不倒裝。

✓ Only when / after / if + 子句 ✓ Only + 介系詞片語 ✓ Only then / only at that moment ✗ Only my boyfriend... → 主詞，不倒裝 ✗ Only Grandma... → 主詞，不倒裝
① 一般動詞 → do/does/did 幫忙
Only [副詞語] + do/does/did + S + 原V
You realize its value only when you lose health.
→ Only when you lose health do you realize its value.
② Be 動詞 → Be 移到主詞前
Only [副詞語] + be + S + ...
Citizens are allowed to vote only after they turn 20.
→ Only after they turn 20 are citizens allowed to vote.
③ 助動詞（can/will/must...）→ 助動詞移到主詞前
Only [副詞語] + 助動詞 + S + 原V
We can succeed only by working hard.
→ Only by working hard can we succeed.
⭐ Not until 同類倒裝（否定字放句首）
I didn't do homework until mom came home.
→ Not until mom came home did I do homework.
倍數放哪裡？

只要把倍數加在比較級前面就好了！

S + V + N times + as 原級 as
Tom is three times as tall as Peter.
Cheetahs run twice as fast as humans.
S + V + N times + 比較級 + than
Tom is three times taller than Peter.
Cheetahs run twice faster than humans.
倍數怎麼說
once ❌（沒有這用法） twice = 2倍 three times = 3倍 four times = 4倍

as...as 中間放原級（不加 -er！）

⭐ 不可數名詞用 much，可數名詞用 many
John has three times as much money as Bill. （錢→不可數）
I will learn four times more words than I learned. （比較級）
推測的強弱程度
must（一定） may（可能） might（可能） could（可能）

⬆ 強 → 弱 ⬇ （must 確定度最高，could 最低）

① 對現在/未來事件推測
S + must/may/might/could + 原V
Ellen is sleeping. She must be very tired.
He raised his hand. He might have a question.
② 對過去事件推測
S + must/may/might/could + have + p.p.
The house was a mess. Someone must have broken in.
I can't find my phone. I could have lost it.
⭐ 常考易混點：現在 vs 過去
進行式推測現在：She must be preparing for a contest. （她正在準備）
過去推測：A thief must have broken into her house. （過去入室行竊）
