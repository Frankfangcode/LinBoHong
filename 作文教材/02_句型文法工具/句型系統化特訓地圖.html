Phase 1：防禦工事 (基礎正確率)

寫作的第一原則：先求不扣分，再求加分。這裡整理了你最容易犯的錯誤。

⚠️ 常見錯誤儀表板 (Top 5 Errors)

錯誤類型 ❌ 你的直覺 (錯誤) ✔ 正確寫法 核心觀念
1. S-V 一致性 The government provide... The government provides... 單數主詞 + 單數動詞 (加s)
2. 不可數名詞 many informations / advices much information / advice 抽象名詞通常不可數，不可加 s
3. 特定動詞用法 I enjoy to swim. I enjoy swimming. enjoy, finish, mind + V-ing
4. Run-on Sentence I like swimming, it is fun. I like swimming because it is fun. 兩個子句不能只用逗號連接
5. 關代誤用 The student which studies hard... The student who studies hard... 人配 who，物配 which

🎯 基礎修復特訓 (3-Stage Flow)

Step 1: 示範 (Modeling)

目標：修正 Run-on Sentence (連寫句)

錯句：Parents go to the park, they want to relax.

✔ Parents go to the park because they want to relax.

解析：加入連接詞 because 連接兩個動作。

Step 2: 半開放練習 (Guided Practice)

請用連接詞修正以下句子：

題目：The weather was bad, we cancelled the trip.

We cancelled the trip the weather was bad.
Ans: because / since / as
Step 3: 獨立任務 (Mission A)

挑戰任務：

請檢查你最近的一篇作文，找出 3 個「S-V 不一致」或「名詞單複數」錯誤並修正。

Phase 2：武器升級 (密度與層次)

目標：拒絕短句。用「關係代名詞」和「名詞子句」讓句子變長、變厚實。

🔧 武器 A：關係代名詞 (Adjective Clause)

合併句子神器
Step 1: 示範

原句 1: The park has a lake.

原句 2: People like to walk around the lake.

→ The park has a lake which people like to walk around.

Step 2: 填空引導

題目：He is the swimmer. He won the gold medal.

He is the swimmer won the gold medal.

Ans: who (因為先行詞是人)
Step 3: 實戰造句

你的任務：

請用 "which" 或 "where" 合併這兩句：
1. I study at NCCU (政大).
2. NCCU has a beautiful library.

NCCU, where I study, has a beautiful library.
(或 NCCU, which has a beautiful library, is where I study.)

🧠 武器 B：名詞子句 (Noun Clause)

觀點表達神器

I believe that... (觀點)

不要只寫 "Sports are good."

→ I believe that sports are beneficial.

What... is... (強調主詞)

不要只寫 "We need patience."

→ What we need is patience.

Mission B: 觀點升級

將句子 "Time management is important." 改寫成名詞子句。

1. I think that time management is important.
2. What is important is time management.

Phase 3：高階連擊 (句型變身)

這一關是分水嶺。我們要學會「同一個意思，四種高級寫法」。這會讓閱卷教授覺得你的英文非常靈活。

✨ 句型變身實驗室

原始句子 (Base Sentence)

The park helps people relax.

1. 關係代名詞變身

插入補充

The park, which helps people relax, is popular.

2. 名詞子句變身

強調事實

What the park does is (to) help people relax.

3. 分詞構句變身

簡潔精練

Helping people relax, the park is indispensable.

(主動用 V-ing, 被動用 p.p.)

4. 強調句變身

語氣加強

It is the park that helps people relax.

🏋️‍♂️ Mission C: 變身訓練

請嘗試將 "Technology changes our lives." 進行「分詞構句」與「強調句」變身。

1. Changing our lives, technology plays a key role.
2. It is technology that changes our lives.

Phase 4：建築藍圖 (滿分模板)

學測作文不需要從零發明。這裡有經過驗證的段落骨架，讓你考試時直接填肉。

📄 Paragraph 1: 圖片描述

[開場] In Picture A, we can see a vivid scene where [描述主詞與動作].

[細節] Surrounded by [背景物件], the character seems to be [情緒/狀態].

[對比] In contrast, Picture B depicts a completely different situation. Here, [描述圖B].

[小結] What makes these two pictures distinct is that [圖A與圖B的核心差異].

💡教練筆記：記得用到 "Surrounded by" (分詞) 和 "What makes..." (名詞子句)。

📝 Paragraph 2: 論說與經驗

[主題句] As for me, I believe that [你的觀點/選擇].

[理由] The main reason is that [解釋原因].

[個人經驗] Take my own experience as an example. I remember a time when [你的故事].

[結論-大招] Only by [你的建議/行動] can we truly [美好結果].

💡 教練筆記：結論句一定要用 Only by 倒裝句，這是拿 18+ 分的關鍵。
