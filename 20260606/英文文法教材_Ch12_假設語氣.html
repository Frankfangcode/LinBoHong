Point 1

形容詞 Adjectives

修飾名詞或代名詞，表達其性質和狀態

（一）形容詞的位置

🔴 放名詞前

She is a friendly lady.
她是個友善的女士。
John is an excellent actor.

🟢 放名詞後（後位修飾）

以下三種情形需後位修飾：

Did you see anything interesting?
修飾 -body/-one/-thing/-where 等不定代名詞
The tower is 100 meters tall.
數字 + 測量單位 + 形容詞
The man who is standing there is my brother.
形容詞子句 / 分詞片語 / 不定詞片語

🔵 放 be 動詞或連綴動詞後

The situation is dangerous.
Our teacher seems angry with us.

🟣 放受詞後（受詞補語）

S + V + O + OC 句型

What he said made me sad.
The earthquake left thousands of people homeless.

⚠️ 窩心叮嚀：a- 開頭的特殊形容詞

以下形容詞只能放在 be 動詞後，不可置於名詞之前！

afraid

害怕的

alike

相像的

alive

活著的

alone

單獨的

asleep

睡著的

awake

醒著的

aware

意識到的

ashamed

感到羞恥的

✅ That baby is asleep.
❌ It is an asleep baby.
（二）數量形容詞

📊 修飾可數名詞

許多；大量一些（很）少
many
quite a few
a good/great many
a large/great number of
a large number of		 a few
several
a number of		 a small number of
few（非常少；幾乎沒有）

📊 修飾不可數名詞

許多；大量一些（很）少
much
a great deal of
quite a little
a large amount of
a large sum of + 金額		 a little a small amount of
a small sum of + 金額
little（很少；幾乎沒有）

📊 修飾可數 AND 不可數

許多；大量一些
a lot of = lots of
plenty of
a large/great quantity of		some

⚠️ 考試重點：the number of vs. a number of

the number of
句型 the number of + 複數名詞 + 單數動詞
The number of people involved in this scandal is increasing.
表「……的數目」→ 視為單數
a number of
句型 a number of + 複數名詞 + 複數動詞
A number of courses are provided for students.
表「一些……」→ 視為複數
（三/四）原級 · 比較級 · 最高級

⚡ 形容詞三級比較

級別使用時機例句
原級單獨描述 / 程度相當Amy is very beautiful.
比較級兩者比較Amy is more beautiful than Sarah.
最高級三者（以上）最高/最低Amy is the most beautiful teacher.

形容詞原級的用法

句型一
正面S + V + as + adj. + as … （和……一樣）
Kelly is as tall as I (am). Kelly 和我一樣高。
句型二
否定S + V + not + as/so + adj. + as … （不如……）
Their house is not as/so big as ours. 他們的房子不如我們的大。
句型三
名詞量S + V + as much/many + N + as …（和……一樣多）
I have as much money as he (does).
I bought as many books as you (did).
句型四
盡可能S + V + as + adj. + as + possible / one can （盡可能……）
We keep the room as clean as possible.
= We keep the room as clean as we can.

形容詞比較級的用法

句型一
比較S + be + adj.-er / more + adj. + than
Health is more important than money.

💡 加強程度

比較級前可加：much, a lot, far, a great deal, even, still
❌ 絕對不可加 very！

句型二
兩者中較S + be + the + 比較級 + of the two
Emily is the thinner of the two girls.

⚠️ 注意！

比較級前一定要加 the！

句型三
越來越adj.-er and adj.-er
more and more + adj.
It's getting hotter and hotter.
The situation is becoming more and more complicated.
句型四
越…越…The + 比較級…, the + 比較級…
The hotter the weather, the lazier we'll get.
The more optimistic you are, the happier you'll be.
句型五
比較（含to）be + superior / inferior / senior / junior + to + 受格
superior to（比……優秀）
inferior to（比……不如）
senior to（比……資深）
junior to（比……資淺）

💡 注意

這些形容詞已含「比較」意思，後面恆用 to + 受格，不用 more/than！

形容詞最高級的用法

句型一
最高級S + V + the + adj.-est/most + adj. + among/of + 範圍 / in + 地點
Jack is by far the smartest student in our class.
可加 by far 加強語氣
Dr. Owl is the best teacher ever.
可加 ever 在最高級後加強
句型二
沒有比…更… No other + N(單數) + V + 比較級 + than…
= S + V + 比較級 + than any other + 單N / all the other + 複N
No other student in Dr. Owl's class is heavier than Piggy.
= Piggy is heavier than any other student in Dr. Owl's class.
小豬比任何其他學生都要重。注意：other 不可去掉！

📋 不規則比較級/最高級

原級比較級最高級
good / wellbetter(the) best
bad / illworse(the) worst
littleless(the) least
many / muchmore(the) most
old（年紀）older(the) oldest
old（排行）elder(the) eldest
far（距離）farther(the) farthest
far（程度/距離）further(the) furthest
late（時間）later(the) latest（最新的）
late（順序）latter(the) last（最後的）

✖️ 倍數的用法

句型一倍數 + as + 原級 + as = 倍數 + 比較級 + than
Our country is ten times as large as yours.
= Our country is ten times larger than yours.
句型二倍數 + the + N + of…（如 the price of / the weight of）
This car is four times the price of that one.

💡 倍數用字

twice（兩倍）, three times, four times, five times…
當倍數為 half 或 twice，搭配比較級 -er 或 more

🎯 形容詞考試重點整理

1
a- 開頭形容詞（afraid, asleep, alive 等）只能放 be 動詞後
2
the number of + 單數動詞 vs. a number of + 複數動詞
3
比較級前絕對不加 very，可加 much/far/a lot/even/still
4
two者中較：S + be + the + 比較級 + of the two（一定要有 the！）
5
superior/inferior/senior/junior 後面用 to，不用 than
6
No other 句型中 other 不可去掉
7
as...as 中間必須用原級；not as/so...as 用於否定句
8
倍數：twice 和 half 搭配比較級；其他倍數（three times...）可搭配原級或比較級

複合形容詞

名詞/形容詞/副詞 與 現在/過去分詞 搭配

⚙️ V-ing vs. p.p. 關鍵規則

V-ing → 主動（令人……的）

a heart-breaking story
= a story that breaks one's heart

p.p. → 被動（感到……的）

a heart-broken man
= a man whose heart has been broken

📋 常見複合形容詞分類

結構例子中文
(1) N-V-ingeye-catching, heart-breaking, energy-saving引人注目的、令人心碎的、節省能源的
(2) N-p.p.hand-made, heart-broken, snow-covered手工的、心碎的、被雪覆蓋的
(3) N-adj.bullet-proof, life-long, earth-friendly防彈的、終身的、環保的
(4) adj.-V-inggood-looking, sweet-smelling, good-tasting好看的、香的、好吃的
(5) adj.-p.p.ready-made, red-painted現成的、漆成紅色的
(6) adj.-N-edlong-haired, one-eyed, kind-hearted長髮的、獨眼的、心腸好的
(7) adv.-V-inghard-working, fast-moving, long-lasting努力工作的、速度快的、持久的
(8) adv.-p.p.beautifully-decorated, well-educated裝飾美麗的、受良好教育的

🎯 複合形容詞考試重點

1
V-ing = 主動（令人…的）；p.p. = 被動（感到…的）
2
good-looking（✅）vs. looking-good（❌） — 形容詞修飾語通常在前
3
old-fashioned（✅）vs. old-fashion（❌） — 後面需加 -ed
4
time-consuming（消耗時間的）、weight-reducing（減重的）— 注意主被動
Point 2

副詞 Adverbs

修飾動詞、形容詞、副詞及整個句子

1. 狀態副詞

表動作或狀態，可放在動詞片語前或動詞後

He ran into the house quickly.
Mom carefully put the vase on the table.

2. 時間副詞

可放句尾或句首，前不加介系詞

We'll move to Taichung next year.

⚠️ this~/next~/last~ 前千萬不可加介系詞！

in next year → next year

3. 地方副詞

常置句尾。常見：here, there, upstairs, downstairs, home, outside, nowhere, everywhere

Let's go outside and get some exercise.

4. 頻率副詞

頻率從低到高：

never → seldom/rarely → sometimes → often → usually → always

📌 位置規則

be動詞後、一般動詞前、助動詞和原V之間

5. 程度副詞

修飾形容詞或副詞的程度

常見：so, too, much, enough, very, quite, pretty, rather, extremely, fairly

⚠️ enough 的位置！

only enough 必須置於被修飾字之後

Sam is not old enough to attend school.

6-7. 疑問副詞 & 關係副詞

疑問副詞：when（時）where（地）why（原因）how（方式）

關係副詞：where, when, why, how

This is where we live.
This is why I like baseball.
狀態副詞的構成法

📐 形容詞 → 副詞

規則例子
形容詞 + -lyactively, badly, carefully, quickly, slowly
字尾 -le → -lygentle→gently, simple→simply, possible→possibly
字尾「子音+y」去y加 -ilyangry→angrily, easy→easily, happy→happily
字尾 -ll + -yfull→fully, dull→dully
字尾 -ic + -allyautomatic→automatically, energetic→energetically
字尾 -ue 去e + -lytrue→truly

⚠️ 字尾 -ly 不一定是副詞！

cost→costly（形容詞）, friend→friendly（形容詞）, time→timely（形容詞）

💡 形副同形

early（早的/早地）、fast（快的/快地）、late（晚的/晚地）、hard（硬的/努力地）

易混淆副詞

⚠️ 易混淆副詞對比

副詞 A中文副詞 B中文
hard努力地hardly幾乎不
high高（地點）highly很；非常
deep深地deeply強烈地；深切地

✅ He works hard every day.（努力）
❌ I studied hardly last night.（應用 hard）
She is highly intelligent.（非常聰明）
The earth's climate has been deeply influenced by global warming.（深切地受影響）
副詞原級・比較級・最高級
副詞比較級
句型S + V + adv.-er / more + adv. + than
Tom drove faster than I (did).
越…越…
句型the + 副詞比較級…, the + 副詞比較級…
The more often you practice, the faster you can run.
副詞最高級
句型S + V + (the) + adv.-est / most + adv.
Chris sings (the) best in his class.
Mary danced (the) most beautifully among us.

🎯 副詞考試重點整理

1
頻率副詞位置：be動詞後、一般動詞前、助動詞和原V之間
2
enough 必須置於被修飾字之後（old enough）
3
this~/next~/last~ 前千萬不加介系詞
4
hard（努力）≠ hardly（幾乎不）；high（高）≠ highly（非常）
5
字尾 -ly 不一定是副詞！friendly, costly, timely 是形容詞

轉折語 Transitional Adverbs

使句子間語意順暢，是作文的好幫手

⚠️ 重要！however / besides 不是連接詞！

副詞轉折語在兩個完整句間使用時，須用分號或句點與前句分開，不可用逗號！

✅ He wants to succeed; however, he doesn't want to work hard.
❌ He wants to succeed, however, he doesn't want to work hard.

📋 順序的轉折語

首先此外；再者最後
first of all
firstly
in the first place
to begin/start with
for one thing…for another		 next
secondly
what's more
in addition
besides
furthermore
moreover
also		 lastly
finally
last but not least

📋 因果的轉折語

因此

therefore, thus, hence, accordingly, consequently, in consequence, as a consequence, as a result

He was lazy and often late for work. As a result, he was fired.

📋 對比的轉折語

語意用法
尚管如此；然而however, nevertheless, nonetheless, still, (and) yet
反而instead, rather
另一方面on the other hand
相較之下in/by contrast, on the other hand
正好相反on the contrary

📋 列舉 & 解釋

例如

for example, for instance
Take...for example = Take...as an example

也就是說；換句話說

that is (to say), in other words, namely

🎯 轉折語考試重點

1
besides, however 等是副詞，不是連接詞，兩句間不可只用逗號！
2
in/by contrast = 對比兩個人/物的差異（兩個比較對象）
on the contrary = 恰恰相反（針對同一件事的否定）
3
Take...for example 是祈使句，句尾需加句號再接下一句
4
such as...不可改成 such...like；as 是固定搭配
L7-1

假設語氣延伸

「要不是…；若非」— 表達遺憾、感嘆之意

📖 假設語氣複習

種類If 子句主要子句
條件句（真實）
現在/習慣/事實		現在式現在式 / will
條件句（未來）現在式will/shall + VR
與現在事實相反過去式（be→were）would/could/should/might + VR
與過去事實相反had + p.p.would/could/should/might + have p.p.
要不是…；若非

🔑 核心句型：If it were/had not been for

與現在事實相反
句型 If it were not for + N,
S + would/could/should/might + VR

等同於：
= But for + N, S + would...
= Without + N, S + would...

If it were not for your help, I would fail my English exams.
= But for your help, I would fail...
= Without your help, I would fail...
與過去事實相反
句型 If it had not been for + N,
S + would/could/should/might + have p.p.

等同於：
= But for + N, S + would have...
= Without + N, S + would have...

If it had not been for your help, I would have failed my English exams.
= But for your help, I would have failed...
假設語氣的倒裝句構

🔄 倒裝變化法

規則：① 砍掉 If ② 把 were / had 移到主詞前面

與現在相反
If it were not for my mom's objection, I would go hiking.
→ Were it not for my mom's objection, I would go hiking.
與過去相反
If it hadn't been for her hard work, Julia wouldn't have passed.
→ Had it not been for her hard work, Julia wouldn't have passed.

🔁 三種等效說法整理

種類 現在（與現在相反） 過去（與過去相反）
If it...not for If it were not for + N If it had not been for + N
But for But for + N（現在/過去皆可，靠主句判斷）
Without Without + N（現在/過去皆可，靠主句判斷）
倒裝 Were it not for + N Had it not been for + N

🎯 假設語氣延伸考試重點

1
時態判斷：主要子句用 would VR → 與現在相反；用 would have p.p. → 與過去相反
2
If it were not for = But for = Without（三者等效）
3
倒裝：砍 if，were/had 提前。Were it not for... / Had it not been for...
4
But for / Without 改用介系詞用法時，時態需自行判斷
5
主句 would/could/should/might + VR（現在）或 + have p.p.（過去）
