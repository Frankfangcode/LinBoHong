一句話總評：字數達標（約 200 字）、四大段落功能齊備，結尾倒裝句也成功從上一篇遷移過來；但題目要求寫「你個人的線上課程經驗」，全文從頭到尾都是泛論，沒有任何一個屬於你自己的具體場景，加上片段句（沒有主詞或動詞的半句話）過多，內容與文法兩項被壓在低分帶。

致命傷：前後矛盾（LOG）

第一段寫 “There is no denying that online class can replace the real class.”（線上課無疑能取代實體課），第二段卻寫 “I don't really like online class.”（我不喜歡線上課）。閱卷老師看到立場自相矛盾，內容分會直接往下壓。整篇只能有一個立場，優點段要寫成「雖然方便，但……」。

04

詳細錯誤訂正

01It had been a long time to have online class during Covid-19.T
➡️During the COVID-19 pandemic, we took online classes for a long time.

時態過去完成式 had been 必須有另一個過去時間點當基準，這裡沒有，用簡單過去式即可。

句型「有一段時間沒／已經很久了」的固定句型是 It has been a long time since + 過去式子句，不是 to have。

02Most of the student may had an experience to skip class.SVPL
➡️Most students have probably had the experience of skipping class.

單複數most of the students（有 of 要加 the＋複數）或直接 most students；不可寫 most of the student。

助動詞may 後面一定接原形動詞：may have，不可 may had。

搭配the experience of + V-ing，不是 to V。

03online class have been extreme convinence to learningSVSP
➡️online classes are extremely convenient for learning

主動詞一致online class（單數）配 have 是錯的；泛指要用複數 online classes are。

詞性修飾形容詞要用副詞：extreme → extremely。

拼字convinence → convenient（形容詞）／convenience（名詞）；此處需形容詞。

介系詞be convenient for，不是 to。

04There is no denying that, online class can replace the real class.LOGP
➡️刪除，或改為：There is no denying that online classes can never fully replace face-to-face ones.

邏輯這句說「線上課能取代實體課」，第二段卻說「我不喜歡線上課、不能見朋友」，立場前後打架。

標點that 引導名詞子句，後面不加逗號。

05It can be very cool idea.ARTWW
➡️It was a real relief for me.

冠詞idea 是可數單數名詞，前面必須有 a：a very cool idea。

語域cool 在學測作文太口語，換成 a great advantage／a real relief。

06We don't need to get up early and need to take a bus or even taxic for long time.SPART
➡️We no longer had to get up early or spend a long time on a crowded bus.

連接詞否定句中並列要用 or，不用 and；且 need to 重複，第二個可省略。

拼字taxic → taxi。

冠詞for a long time，不可省略 a。

07In other hand. This method should have a big problem.WWP
➡️On the other hand, this method also has serious drawbacks.

片語固定寫法是 On the other hand（不能用 In，也不能少 the）。

標點轉承語後面接逗號，不是句號。

用字should 是「應該」，此處要陳述事實，用 has／does have。

08Every students will not study hard and won't listen to teacher.PLART
➡️Not every student studies hard or listens to the teacher.

單複數every 後面永遠接單數名詞：every student。

部分否定「不是每個人都…」要寫 Not every student…；寫成 Every student will not… 語意會變成「每個人都不」。

冠詞listen to the teacher。

09When during the exam. Students should cheat on test.FRAGWW
➡️During exams, some students may even cheat on tests.

片段句When 是連接詞，後面必須接主詞＋動詞；When during 兩個時間詞疊在一起，且整句沒有主要子句。

用字should（應該作弊？）語意錯誤，要用 may／might。

10From my perspective., I firmly believe that. I don't really like online class.FRAGP
➡️From my perspective, I firmly believe that online learning can never take the place of the classroom.

片段句believe that 後面必須接完整句子，不能句點結束。

標點perspective 後面出現「., 」兩個標點，只留逗號。

11You can't meet with friends, and just only looking at the camera.FRAGCH
➡️You cannot meet your friends in person; instead, you simply stare at a screen.

對等連接and 前後動詞形式要一致：can't meet … and can only look…；looking 不是動詞形式。

贅字just 與 only 意思重複，留一個。

用字meet with 多指正式會談，同學朋友用 meet／hang out with。

12Speaking from microphone to answer the question.FRAGPREP
➡️You have to speak into a microphone just to answer a question.

片段句整句沒有主詞與主要動詞，只是一個分詞片語。

介系詞對著麥克風說話是 speak into a microphone。

13you may get bad scoreARTCH
➡️you may end up with poor grades / fall behind in class

冠詞score 可數，至少要寫 a bad score／bad scores。

道地度成績不好慣用 poor grades／low grades。

14looking to 3C produces for too long to damage your eyesWWCH
➡️staring at electronic devices for too long damages your eyes

用字「看著」是 look at／stare at，look to 是「指望、求助於」。

中式英文「3C 產品」是台灣特有說法，英文用 electronic devices／digital screens；produce 是「農產品」，產品是 products。

句型誤用too…to… 表「太…以致於不能」（too tired to walk）；此處要表「因為太久而傷眼」，直接用 for too long + 動詞即可。

15only by striking a balance between students and 3C produce can we have a good study effectLOGCH
➡️only by striking a balance between online convenience and face-to-face interaction can we make the most of our learning

倒裝句型正確，扣分在內容：between A and B 的 A、B 必須是可以互相權衡的兩件事；students 和 3C 產品不對等。

中式英文study effect 不存在，學習成效是 learning outcomes／study efficiency。

05

高分優化建議

切題度：整篇最大的漏洞

  • 提示白紙黑字寫「你個人有哪些正面與／或負面的線上課程經驗」，你寫的卻是「大家可能會蹺課、可能會作弊」——這是別人的事，不是你的經驗，內容分因此只能給 2 分。
  • 個人經驗要有時間、科目、動作、結果四要素。例：During the lockdown in 2021, I once fell asleep in a math class with my camera off, and no one noticed until the midterm. 一個具體場景抵十句泛論。
  • 第二段是「整體感想」，不要再重複第一段的缺點清單；要寫「回頭看，我學到了什麼」。可用 Looking back, … 開頭。

組織：兩個馬上可修的毛病

  • 用了 First 就要有 Second／Besides；否則閱卷判定轉承語使用欠妥（這是連續兩篇的同一個問題）。
  • 正面經驗與負面經驗不要塞在同一段的前後半而沒有明顯分界；用 At first… / Before long, however, … 做時間轉折，段落會立刻清楚。
  • 片段句（沒有主詞或動詞的半句）本篇出現 4 次。檢查訣竅：每個句點前，先確認「誰＋做什麼」都在。

句型升級：三個寫「個人經驗」超好用的句子

  • Not until… 倒裝寫轉折： Not until my grades dropped did I realize how little I had learned online.
  • What 名詞子句寫感受： What frustrated me most was staring at a screen full of black squares.
  • While 對比正負面： While online classes saved me an hour of commuting, they also cost me the chance to talk with my classmates.
06

滿分示範改寫

保留你原本的核心想法：方便、不用早起通勤／難專心、看不到同學、傷眼睛／最後以平衡作結。差別在於全部改成「我的」經驗。

When the pandemic hit in 2021, my school suddenly moved every class online. At first, I found it a real relief. I no longer had to get up at six or spend an hour on a crowded bus, and I could replay the recorded lessons whenever a concept confused me. Before long, however, the downside appeared. Sitting alone in front of a screen, I found it almost impossible to concentrate; during one math class, I even fell asleep with my camera off. Since my teacher could not see my face, nobody noticed that I had fallen behind until the midterm results came out.

Looking back, I firmly believe that online learning is a useful tool but never a replacement for the classroom. What I missed most was not the lessons themselves but the people — the chats during breaks and the chance to raise my hand and get an immediate answer. Staring at electronic devices for hours also left my eyes sore and my mind exhausted. To sum up, only by striking a balance between online convenience and face-to-face interaction can we truly make the most of our learning.

約 190 words ｜ 兩段功能分明 ｜ 含分詞構句、What 名詞子句、倒裝句

本回中譯英（機車駕照）

這次只收到作文，沒有學生的翻譯作答。以下先提供標準答案與拆解，可以當堂上口頭練習：先讓學生翻，再對照。

Q1

很多臺灣的青少年，滿十八歲後做的第一件事，就是去考機車駕照。

➡️For many teenagers in Taiwan, the first thing they do after turning eighteen is to take the scooter license test.

滿十八歲turn eighteen／reach the age of eighteen；不要寫 full eighteen。

句型The first thing (that) they do is (to) V — 主詞是 thing，動詞用 is，不可用 are。

考駕照take／get a driver's license test；機車在台灣多為 scooter，motorcycle 亦可。

Q2

然而根據統計，新手的機車騎士，在第一年發生車禍的機率比較高。

➡️However, according to statistics, novice motorcyclists are more likely to be involved in accidents during their first year on the road.

根據統計according to statistics（複數、不加 the）。

新手騎士novice／inexperienced riders／beginning motorcyclists。

機率比較高be more likely to V 或 have a higher chance of V-ing；不要寫 the probability is more high。
