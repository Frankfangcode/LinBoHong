講到心坎裡的手法技巧

【如何破題】

用破題句吸睛勾人心

萬事起頭難,英文作文也如此;如果能成功破題,接下來就好寫了。而作文能取得高分,打造吸引人的開頭是一個訣竅,英文寫作的起手式稱之為「writing hook」,就是所謂的「破題技巧」。破題句(hook sentence)亦是開場句(opening sentence),「hook」的意思有二:首先,指的是在文章開頭的句子能夠像鉤子(hook)一樣鉤住讀者的注意力;再者,是希望這些 hook sentences 能像是拳擊手的鉤拳(hook),有力地敲擊讀者心靈。因此,許多國外英文作家也稱這種破題法為「attention-getter」,亦即吸引讀者注意的句子。

破題句在作文中的位置

Hook sentence (破題句)

"How can a writer expect to get a reader's attention without a good hook?"

Background (說明承接)

"A hook is the line or lines written to lure a reader in and make them want to learn more. It is an introduction that is meant to grab hold of people's attention."

Thesis statement (主旨句)

"Writing a good hook is not always easy, but it is an important skill to learn if we want to write effectively."

顧名思義，破題句就是在開頭段落（Introductory paragraph）的首句。

1. 場景破題法 (Setting Hook)

目的是要為讀者打造一個場景,利用人、事、物的細節描述,激發視覺、聽覺、嗅覺、觸覺、味覺等感受,讓人一開始就有身歷其境之感。

"Under the darkened sky, the sea was white, running sidewise, exploding in sheets of spray against the long arm of land that formed their end of the little bay."

- From The Eyes of the Amaryllis《阿瑪麗麗絲號》by Natelie Babbitt

"In a hole in the ground there lived a hobbit. Not a nasty, dirty, wet hole, filled with the ends of worms and an oozy smell, nor yet a dry, bare, sandy hole with nothing in it to sit down on or to eat: it was a hobbit-hole, and that means comfort."

- From The Hobbit《哈比人》by J.R.R. Tolkien

2. 對話破題法 (Dialogue Hook)

用人物的對白開場,可能是兩人間的對話或是一人的獨白,同時描述人物動作、讓讀者有臨場感,也為後面發生的事件或想法埋下伏筆。

""Where's Papa going with that ax?" said Fern to her mother as they were setting the table for breakfast."

- From Charlotte's Web《夏綠蒂的網》by E.B. White

""To be born again," sang Gibreel Farishta tumbling from the heavens, "first you have to die.""

- From The Satanic Verses《魔鬼詩篇》by Salman Rushdie

3. 名言佳句破題法 (Quotation Hook)

用名人的話語破題,塑造出專業與權威,藉以提升內容的說服力。

""If you want to bring happiness to the whole world, go home, and love your family." Maria Theresa was a smart woman with an amazing mindset. Her popular quote reminds us that there is nothing more important than family."

""It always seems impossible, until it's done." This is a quote from Nelson Mandela, who symbolized perfectly perseverance and hard work."

4. 擬聲詞破題法 (Sound Hook)

透過擬聲詞能即時在腦海顯現動態的感受,創造身歷其境的效果,重現聲音發生時的情景。

"Woosh! Went the wind as my feet touched the soft sand. I was exploring at the beach and was having a great time. It was a beautiful day, the trees swayed in the wind, and the gulls soared through the sky."

- From The Most Wonderful Writing Lessons Ever by Barbara Mariconda

"Chug, chug, chug. Puff, puff, puff. Ding-dong, ding-dong. The little train rumbled over the tracks."

- From The Little Engine That Could《可能的小引擎》by Watty Piper

5. 設問破題法 (Question Hook)

從一個問句寫起以引起讀者注意,將本來平鋪直敘的描述改為疑問句,激發讀者的好奇,再引出主題,也希望透過提問促進讀者深思。

"What is the charm of necklaces? Why would anyone put something extra around their neck and then invest it with special significance? A necklace doesn't afford warmth in cold weather, like a scarf, or protection in combat, like chain mail; it only decorates."

- From On Necklaces by Emily R. Grosholz

"Is virtual learning here to stay for K-12? As students return to post-pandemic classrooms, many are wondering if remote learning will remain an option-and to what extent its impact will be felt."

- From EdTech: Focus on K-12 by Rebecca Torchia

師父有交代：進階篇

對於前面介紹的五種破題寫作技巧若已游刃有餘,不妨再挑戰以下五種進階的破題方式。

1. 事實/數據破題法 (Fact/Statistic Hook)

提出引人注目的事實或數據作為開場,介紹奇趣新知或提出量化資訊,以增加說服力並使人印象深刻,會想繼續讀下去。

"More than half of Iceland's population believes that elves exist or that they possibly can exist. Although this might sound strange to foreigners, many of us have similar beliefs that would sound just as strange to those outside our cultures."

- From More Than Half of This Country Believes in Elves by Rich Warren

"Of about 3 million youth that are injured every year playing sports or participating in recreational activities, only 25% are treated in hospital emergency rooms."

- From Sports Injury Statistics by Stanford Children's Health

2. 懸疑破題法 (Surprise Hook)

從事件的懸疑處寫起,營造神秘氣氛,激發讀者對未知事物的好奇和關注。

"Lydia is dead. But they don't know this yet."

- From Everything I Never Told You《無聲告白》by Celeste Ng

"A screaming comes across the sky. It has happened before, but there is nothing to compare it to now."

- From Gravity's Rainbow《萬有引力之虹》by Thomas Pynchon

3. 小故事破題法 (Story Hook)

利用個人的人生經歷或某個片段的回憶,帶出主要事件的人物、時空背景。

"Finally the day had arrived. I was on my way to Aspen, Colorado. I had heard wonderful stories about the Aspen Music School from friends who had attended in previous years, and I was certain that this summer would be an unbelievable learning experience. I was especially excited to be studying with Mr. Herbert Stessin, an esteemed professor from the Juilliard School."

- From A Lesson About Life by Aaron Miller

"On the morning of October 30, 1969, the body of Chase Andrews lay in the swamp, which would have absorbed it silently, routinely. Hiding it for good. A swamp knows about death, and doesn't necessarily define it as tragedy, certainly not a sin."

- From Where the Crawdads Sing by Delia Owens

4. 一字破題法 (One Word Hook)

用一個英文字開頭,簡潔扼要地下關鍵字,點出心境、狀態;或者先下一個簡短的結論,接著帶出後續的事件發展。

"Justice? You get justice in the next world, in this world you have the law."

- From A Frolic of His Own《他自己的嬉戲》by William Gaddis

"Athlete. Often the word conjures up the image of a lithe, muscled person with one percent body fat, and able to leap tall buildings in a single bound. I am not this person. In fact, my career as an athlete is not what most consider successful."

- From Between a Rock and Home Plate by Mark K. Arimoto

5. 開門見山破題法 (Statement Hook)

開場直接揭露文章的核心概念,由於挑明了說,所以寫來理充詞沛,容易留下深刻的印象。

"It was love at first sight."

- From Catch-22《第22條軍規》by Joseph Heller

"It is a truth universally acknowledged, that a single man in possession of a good fortune, must be in want of a wife."

- From Pride and Prejudice《傲慢與偏見》by Jane Austen

換你試試看 G：評鑑開場句

請閱讀下列關於「防疫停課」的開場句，哪個寫法比較吸引人？在吸睛指數欄位裡給予1~3顆星，並閱讀原因說明。

開場句 吸睛指數

"In this year, Covid-19 spread all over the world, and Taiwan was no exception. Taiwan's government announced a level-three alert due to Covid-19. Everyone needed to stay at home."

原因: 這是平鋪直敘的寫法，只交代了事實，沒有太多情感或懸念，比較難吸引讀者繼續閱讀。
★☆☆

"During the time when I was learning from home, I had to overcome some challenges. For me, compared to our regular school days, it was more difficult to stay at home, since I needed to spend time getting accustomed to studying without my friends around me."

原因: 雖然點出了挑戰，但語氣比較平淡，是比較個人、內心式的開頭，讀者需要多一點耐心才能進入狀況。
★★☆

"Can you imagine staying at home for three months without going to school? It used to be my dream. However, little did I expect that the dream would become a reality due to a virus. Because of the Covid-19 epidemic, I experienced a lockdown for more than 100 days."

原因: 運用了設問破題法，直接與讀者對話，並製造出「夢想成真卻非好事」的反差感，成功勾起讀者的好奇心。
★★★
