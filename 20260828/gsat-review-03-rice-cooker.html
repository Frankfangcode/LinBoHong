一句話總評：字數達標、有清楚的立場，First / Second / Third 這次終於成對出現（前兩篇的目標達成）；但第一段沒有真正寫出「支持判決那一方的理由」，題目要求的雙方論述只完成一半，加上結論句跑到「政府與公司」「貧窮比例」完全離題，四項全部落在低分帶。

致命傷一：雙方理由只有一方

你寫「These two sides may have their reasons.」之後給了 First 和 Second 兩個理由——但這兩個理由（東西丟了就不屬於任何人／法律沒說不能拿）都是在幫清潔隊員說話，支持判決那一方的理由一個都沒有。題目第一段的核心要求就是「雙方的立場與其理由」，這裡直接失分一半。

致命傷二：結論離題

a balance between the government and the company — 這個事件裡沒有任何公司；decrease the percent of the poor（降低窮人比例）也和「法律 vs 人情」無關。倒裝句型是對的，但填進去的內容整段跑掉，閱卷會判定「與主題無關」。

04

詳細錯誤訂正

01The end of 2025, a cleaner gave a electronic pot to a women.MISSART
➡️At the end of 2025, a sanitation worker gave an old electric rice cooker to a woman who collected recyclables.

缺介系詞時間片語必須寫 At the end of 2025；直接以名詞片語開頭句子不成立。

冠詞a → an（electronic 以母音開頭）。

用字electronic＝電子的（電腦、手機）；電鍋是 electric rice cooker。pot 是鍋子／花盆，電鍋要寫 rice cooker。

單複數a women 錯，a woman（單數）／women（複數）。清潔隊員用 sanitation worker 比 cleaner 精確。

02But this action was not allowed.LOG
➡️However, he was later reported and even prosecuted, which sparked heated debate.

內容不足題目的關鍵事實是「遭檢舉並被起訴」，was not allowed 太籠統，讀者看不出事情有多嚴重，也交代不出後面為何會有兩派意見。

語域But 連用兩次放句首，改用 However／Nevertheless 較正式。

03Some of the people though the law was correct.SPPL
➡️Some people argue that the ruling was made strictly according to the law.

拼字though（雖然）→ thought（認為）。這兩個字差一個字母但詞性完全不同，是高頻扣分點。

用法泛指「有些人」直接用 some people，不加 of the。

精確度the law was correct 太模糊；題目講的是「判決依法辦理」＝ the ruling followed the law。

04But other people thought it was too strict, couldn't hold real condition on the first sight.FRAGCH
➡️Others, however, question whether the law is too rigid to take human kindness and real situations into account.

句構逗號後直接接 couldn't，這個子句沒有主詞，變成破碎句；應加 and it／which，或改成不定詞片語。

中式英文hold real condition 是「握住真實情況」，中文的「兼顧」英文用 take … into account／balance／consider。

用字on the first sight 應為 at first sight，但此處語意不合，直接刪除。

05First, the thing they don't want, and then throw away. It may belongs noone.FRAGSV
➡️First, once people throw something away, it no longer belongs to anyone.

片段句「the thing they don't want, and then throw away」只有名詞片語，沒有主要動詞，整句不成立。

動詞形式may 後面接原形：may belong，不可 may belongs。

搭配＋拼字belong 一定接 to：belong to no one；noone 不是英文字，要分開寫 no one。

06Second the law didn't said that the recycle can't take away under agree.TCH
➡️Second, the law does not clearly state that recyclables cannot be given away with the owner's consent.

動詞形式didn't 後面接原形 say，不可 didn't said。

詞性recycle 是動詞（回收）；「回收物」名詞是 recyclables／recycled items。

語態回收物是「被拿走」，須用被動 cannot be taken away。

中式英文under agree 不成詞；「經同意」＝ with consent／with permission。

標點Second 後面要加逗號。

07I don't agree with the news.WW
➡️I cannot agree with the ruling / the court's decision.

用字你不同意的是「判決」，不是「新聞」本身；news 是報導這件事的媒體內容。

08I don't thik the law had consider of the citizen.SPT
➡️I don't think the law takes ordinary citizens into consideration.

拼字thik → think。

動詞形式had consider 錯誤；完成式須用過去分詞 had considered，但此處在談現況，用現在式 takes 即可。

搭配consider 是及物動詞，後面不加 of；或用 take … into consideration。

09so that means anyone who want can take it awaySVCH
➡️which means anyone who wants it can take it away

關係代名詞要承接前面「整句話」，用 which means；so that means 是中文「所以那意思是」的直譯。

主動詞一致先行詞 anyone 是單數，關係子句動詞要加 s：who wants；且 want 是及物動詞，需受詞 it。

10Second, not everyone have enough budget to have an electronic.SVWW
➡️Second, not everyone can afford to buy a new appliance.

主動詞一致everyone／not everyone 都視為單數，動詞要用 has。

用字budget 指預算（公司、政府編列），個人「買不起」用 cannot afford。

詞性electronic 是形容詞，不能單獨當名詞；家電是 an appliance／an electrical appliance。

11Third, I think the law is just a paper. On the paper, their have all lot of thing can't write completely.ARTSP
➡️Third, the law exists only on paper; no written rule can cover every situation in real life.

慣用語a paper 是「一張紙／一篇論文」；「紙上談兵、只存在於紙面」固定說 on paper（不加冠詞）。

拼字their（他們的）→ there；「有…」的句型是 there are。

用字all lot of → a lot of；thing 要複數 things。

中式英文can't write completely 是「寫不完整」的直譯；英文要說「法條無法涵蓋所有情況」＝ cannot cover every situation。

12Only by striking a balance between the gonvernment and the company, can decrease the percent of the poor and make the society better.LOGMISS
➡️Only by striking a balance between the letter of the law and human compassion can we build a society that is both orderly and warm.

離題本案的兩方是「法律條文」與「人情／實際情況」，不是政府與公司；「降低窮人比例」也不是本題要談的結論。結論一定要扣回題目的爭點。

缺主詞倒裝句 Only by… can + 主詞 + 原形動詞；你少了 we，變成沒有主詞的句子。

標點Only by 片語與倒裝的主要子句之間不加逗號。

拼字＋冠詞gonvernment → government；society 泛指時不加 the。

高分優化建議

第一段：議論題的「雙方」寫法模板

背景一句 → 正方立場＋理由兩句 → 反方立場＋理由兩句。兩邊的句數要對稱，這樣閱卷一眼就看得出你寫齊了。

支持判決（你完全沒寫）

  • 回收物一旦被清潔隊收走，即屬公有財產，公務員不得私自處分。
  • 若人人可自行決定送誰，制度將難以管理，也可能衍生私相授受。
  • 句型：Those who support the ruling argue that…

質疑法律過嚴（你寫的方向）

  • 那只是被丟棄的舊電鍋，沒有任何人受損失。
  • 法律若毫無裁量空間，就無法兼顧人情與實際情況。
  • 句型：Others, however, contend that…

切題度：三個非寫不可的關鍵字

  • 提示裡的 「遭檢舉並被起訴」（was reported and prosecuted）、「依法辦理」（followed the law）、「人情與實際情況」（human kindness and real circumstances）——這三個關鍵詞的英文一定要出現在文章裡，這是閱卷判斷你「有沒有讀懂題目」的直接證據。
  • 動筆前先做 30 秒：把提示每一句圈出關鍵詞，寫在稿紙邊，寫完檢查每個關鍵詞有沒有被用到。這是你三篇以來最需要建立的習慣。

句型升級：議論文三把刀

  • 對立句： Those who support the ruling argue that…, while others contend that the law is too rigid.
  • 讓步句： It is true that rules must be followed; however, a law without room for discretion may do more harm than good.
  • No matter how 讓步： No matter how carefully a law is written, it can never cover every situation in real life.
06

滿分示範改寫

保留你原本的核心想法：東西已被丟棄、不是人人買得起、法條無法涵蓋所有情況、最後以平衡作結。差別在於補上正方理由，並把結論拉回「法律 vs 人情」。

At the end of 2025, a sanitation worker in Taipei gave an old electric rice cooker, which someone had thrown away, to a woman who made a living collecting recyclables. He was later reported and even prosecuted, and the case soon sparked heated debate. Those who support the ruling argue that once garbage is collected, it becomes public property, and no public employee should decide privately what to do with it; otherwise, the whole system would be impossible to manage. Others, however, contend that the law is far too rigid. In their eyes, an abandoned rice cooker harmed no one, and a law that ignores human kindness and real circumstances is hard to accept.

From my perspective, the worker deserves sympathy rather than punishment. First, the cooker had already been thrown away, which means its original owner lost nothing at all. Second, not everyone can afford to buy a new appliance, and giving the cooker a second life helped both the woman and the environment. Third, no matter how carefully a law is written, it can never cover every situation in real life; that is exactly why discretion matters. To sum up, only by striking a balance between the letter of the law and human compassion can we build a society that is both orderly and warm.
