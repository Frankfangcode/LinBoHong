Part 1 · L4 複合形容詞

一個問題決定答案：「後面的名詞」自己做，還是被做？

① 有動作 → 主動用 V-ing，被動用 p.p.

原本的句子複合形容詞為什麼
a car that moves fasta fast-moving car車自己動 → V-ing
a machine that saves timea time-saving machine機器自己省時間 → V-ing
a dog that is trained wella well-trained dog狗被訓練 → p.p.
vegetables grown at homehome-grown vegetables菜被種 → p.p.

② 數字 + 名詞 → 名詞用單數

a five-star hotel　a twenty-story building　a ten-page report　a three-year-old boy
中間的名詞是「單位」，不加 -s。

③ 外觀、個性 → 形容詞／數字 + N-ed

long-haired　red-haired　three-legged　kind-hearted　open-minded　bad-tempered
意思是「有……的」：a girl with long hair = a long-haired girl。

④ 看起來／聞起來／嚐起來 → 形容詞 + V-ing

nice-looking　sweet-smelling　good-tasting　silly-looking
a dish that tastes good → a good-tasting dish（不是 good-tasted）

⑤ 其他固定組合

名詞 + 形容詞：sugar-free　user-friendly　world-famous
被動狀態：ready-made（現成的）　red-painted
多字組成：an all-you-can-eat restaurant　a once-in-a-lifetime experience
兩個特殊考點
① strike 當「受災難、疾病所苦」時，p.p. 變成 stricken：drought-stricken、poverty-stricken、flood-stricken。
② -ly 副詞後面通常不加連字號：a newly built skyscraper。
Part 2 · L5 as if 彷彿、好像

只要問：as if 後面那件事，跟前面「同時」還是「更早」？

① 同時發生 → as if + 過去式／were

He talks as if he knew everything.（現在講得好像什麼都知道）
He talked as if he knew everything.（那時講得好像什麼都知道）
be 動詞不管主詞是誰，一律用 were：Katie looks as if she were ill.

② 更早發生 → as if + had + p.p.

He talks as if he had known the news yesterday.
Katie looked as if she had been ill.（看起來好像之前生過病）
常見錯誤　前面用 talks 或 talked 都不影響判斷，只看時間先後。
had 後面一定要接 p.p.：had solve ✗ → had solved ✓
Part 3 · L5–L6 假設語氣 · 倒裝 · 混合

先判斷時間，再決定動詞

① 三種假設語氣

跟什麼相反if 子句主要子句
現在If I were you, / If I won…would／could + VR
過去If I had taken…would／could + have p.p.
未來（不太可能）If Peter were to ask me out…would／could + VR

② 倒裝：拿掉 if，把 were／had 移到主詞前面

If I were you → Were I you
If I had known → Had I known
If it were not for… → Were it not for…（要不是…，現在）
If it had not been for… → Had it not been for…（要不是…，過去）

③ 混合句：過去的條件 → 現在的結果

If he had taken the advice then, he would be fine now.
看到句子裡有 then／last year 和 now／today 同時出現，就要想到混合句。

④ wish 假設語氣

I wish I were tall.（現在）　I wish I had told her the truth.（過去）
陷阱　不是每個 if 都是假設！If it rains tomorrow, we will stay home. 這是真的可能發生的條件句，用現在式＋will。
另外：If I had a wings ✗ → If I had wings ✓（wings 是複數，不加 a）
Part 4 · 錯題回練

上次寫錯的類型，換句再練一次

  1. We must control the fast-spreading fire. (a fire that spreads fast)
  2. It was a fast-growing company. (a company that grows fast)
  3. She lives in a ten-story building. (a building with ten stories)
  4. The boy acted as if he were a king last night. (be)
  5. Mia talks as if she were my mom, but she isn't. (be)
  6. Leo talks as if he had finished the homework, but he hasn't. (finish)
  7. Tom looked pale, as if he had seen a ghost. (see)
  8. If I had wings, I would fly to you. (have)
  9. Had I studied harder, I would have passed the test.
  10. Were I you, I would say sorry to her.
