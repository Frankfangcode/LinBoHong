中譯英批改（8 分）

Q1 ｜ 得分 1.5 / 4

因應臺灣人口老化問題，政府已經提供長照服務以減少家庭的負擔。

To face the question of the older, government has required long-term severices to reduce the heavy of families.

➡️In response to Taiwan's aging population, the government has provided long-term care services to reduce the burden on families.

因應「因應／為了應對」固定說法是 In response to；To face 是中式直譯。

人口老化不是 the question of the older，而是 the aging population／the problem of an aging population，且不可漏掉 Taiwan's。

提供required（要求）＝ 用錯字，應為 provided／offered；長照＝ long-term care services，severices 為拼字錯誤。

負擔heavy 是形容詞不能當名詞；「減少家庭的負擔」＝ reduce the burden on families（burden 後接 on）。

Q2 ｜ 得分 2 / 4

當年長者不再能獨立生活時，他們需要各式各樣的服務選項在日常生活中協助他們。

不再能can't 只翻到「不能」，漏掉「不再」；用 can no longer 才完整。

04

詳細錯誤訂正

01The picture illustrate a place
➡️The picture illustrates / shows / depicts a lively scene

主動詞一致The picture 為第三人稱單數，動詞須加 -s。

02a place, where is known for Taiwanese
➡️a landmark that is well known to Taiwanese

關係詞where 是關係副詞，後面須接完整句；此處缺主詞，要用 that／which。

片語be known for ＝以…聞名（後接特色）；be known to ＝為某人所熟知（後接人）。

03In the begin of the year 2024
➡️At the beginning of 2024

詞性＋介系詞begin 是動詞，名詞為 beginning；且固定用 at the beginning of。

04a "Yellow Duck" appeared at Kioshong
➡️a giant yellow rubber duck made its debut in Kaohsiung

專有名詞高雄＝ Kaohsiung，拼錯專有名詞在詞彙項會直接扣分。

介系詞城市前用 in，不用 at。「黃色小鴨」英文慣稱 rubber duck。

05attracted a lot of people to take a picture with it
➡️attracted crowds of visitors, who lined up to take photos with it

單複數很多人拍的照片不會只有一張，用 photos；並可加關係子句補圖片細節。

06Because of it cute look, it famous for not only adults but also children.
➡️Thanks to its adorable appearance, it became popular with both adults and children.

所有格it（它）→ its（它的）。

缺動詞famous 是形容詞，句子沒有動詞；須寫 it was famous／it became popular。

搭配詞be famous for ＋事物；受某人歡迎要用 be popular with／be a hit among。

07the duck can be the presence of Kioshong
➡️the duck can serve as a symbol of Kaohsiung

用字presence ＝存在感／出席，不能表「代表」；應用 symbol／icon／representative。

08the traviler post the pictures upload the internet
➡️travelers post the photos they take on social media

拼字traviler → traveler／traveller。

一句一動詞post 與 upload 兩個動詞並排是中式英文；擇一即可，或用 and 連接。

09When others saw it on the news, they will surf the key words
➡️When others see it in the news, they will search for the keywords online

時態一致主要子句用 will（未來），附屬子句不可用過去式 saw；時間副詞子句用現在式代替未來式。

用字surf 只能接 the Internet／the web；「搜尋關鍵字」用 search for／google。keyword 為一個字。

10Then, it will more and more people knows it.
➡️As a result, more and more people will get to know the city.

句構崩壞it will 後面沒有動詞，又多出一個主詞 people；一句只能有一組主詞＋動詞。

單複數people 為複數，動詞不加 s。

11In contrast, it also bring backward too.
➡️On the other hand, such popularity may also come at a price.

轉承語In contrast 用於「兩個對象互相對比」；同一件事的另一面要用 On the other hand／However。

用字backward ＝向後的（形容詞），「缺點」是 drawbacks／downsides。

贅字also 與 too 意思重複，留一個就好；且 it 後動詞須 brings。

12The over tourism and trash pollution will also make the city look bad.
➡️Overtourism and littering can easily tarnish the city's image.

構詞overtourism 是一個字，且為不可數，前面不加 The。

道地度trash pollution 為自創詞，應用 littering／garbage problems；make the city look bad 太口語，改 damage／tarnish the city's image 更符合題目「城市形象」。

13a beautiful place to abroad
➡️a destination truly worth visiting

詞性abroad 是副詞（go abroad），不能接在 to 之後；且本題寫的是台灣的城市，用 abroad 語意也不合。

05

高分優化建議

切題度：第一段「看圖說話」幾乎沒寫

  • 題目明講要寫「人、地、物及活動」，四項你只碰到「物（小鴨）」。圖中成群的民眾、高舉手機自拍、背景高雄流行音樂中心的貝殼狀建築、海港全部沒出現，這是內容掉到 3 分的關鍵。
  • 第一段的黃金公式：大景（地點）→ 中景（主角物件）→ 近景（人物動作）→ 整體氛圍，一句一層，四句就是 60 字。
  • 第二段「影響」正反比例失衡：正面只有「被更多人知道」一個點，缺少帶動觀光收益／商家生意；建議正面兩點、負面一點，最後再收尾。

組織：轉承語要成對出現

  • 用了 For one thing 就一定要有 For another，否則閱卷老師會判定「轉承語使用欠妥」。
  • 第二段開頭先寫 From my perspective, I firmly believe… 是好習慣（主題句），但接著馬上跳到旅客貼照片，中間缺一句銜接。
  • 結論句 To sum up 之後直接接倒裝句，收得漂亮，這個模式請固定下來當作你的結尾模板。

滿分示範改寫

保留你原本的核心想法：小鴨可以成為城市象徵、網路傳播帶來曝光、過度觀光有代價、最後以平衡作結。

The picture shows a lively scene by the harbor in Kaohsiung, a city well known to almost every Taiwanese. At the beginning of 2024, a giant yellow rubber duck made its debut there, floating peacefully in front of the curved buildings of the Music Center. A sea of visitors of all ages gathered along the waterfront: some were pointing at the duck excitedly, while others raised their phones to snap photos with it. Thanks to its adorable appearance and round, innocent eyes, the duck quickly became a hit not only among adults but also among children.

From my perspective, artworks like this rubber duck can serve as a powerful symbol of a city. For one thing, travelers post their photos on social media, and once the news spreads, more and more people search for the keywords online and put the city on their must-visit lists. For another, the crowds they attract bring considerable business to local shops, restaurants, and hotels. On the other hand, such popularity may also come at a price, since overtourism and littering can easily tarnish the city's image. To sum up, only by striking a balance between tourism and the environment can we turn a temporary sensation into a destination truly worth visiting.
