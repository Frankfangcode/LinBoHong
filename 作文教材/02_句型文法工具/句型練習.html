Phase 1：基礎句型正確化

目標：把錯誤率降到最低，避免 run-on sentence、主詞動詞不一致、冠詞複數錯誤。

做到這裡 → 作文穩定 14–16 分。

學習內容（必練）

1. 基本句型五大類（正確寫出 S + V、S + V + O）

  • S + V (The sun rises.)
  • S + V + O (The government provides various facilities.)
  • S + V + C (The park is full of trees.)
  • S + V + IO + DO (He gave me a book.)
  • S + V + O + OC (We consider him a leader.)

2. 避免句子過長 → 拆句練習（重要！）

這能完全消除你文章中的 run-on sentence（連寫句）。

❌ 錯誤範例 (Run-on)：

Parents go to the park, they want to relax, they are tired.

✔ 正確範例 (複句)：

Parents go to the park because they want to relax.

✔ 正確範例 (拆句)：

Parents go to the park. They want to relax because they are tired.

3. 冠詞 / 複數 / 動詞一致性

每日練 5 句 → 查錯誤 → 改寫。

Phase 1 練習任務（基礎抓錯）

找出以下句子中的基礎錯誤並修正：

1. People is worried about the future.

答案：

People are worried... (主詞動詞不一致：People 是複數)

2. He bought many book yesterday.

答案：

...many books... (複數錯誤：many 後面接複數名詞)

3. I went to store, I bought milk.

答案：

I went to the store and I bought milk. (冠詞錯誤 + Run-on Sentence)

或：I went to the store. I bought milk.

🎯 實戰演練與考試應用 (Phase 1)

🧠 考試怎麼用？(基礎)

這是您的「防扣分網」。在考場上，每寫完一個段落，您只有 30 秒能檢查。請「只」檢查三件事：

  • S-V 一致：He go -> goes?
  • 複數 (N)：Many student -> students?
  • 時態 (V)：Yesterday ... go -> went?

只要守住這三點，您的基礎分（14分）就穩了。

更多基礎抓錯練習：

4. There are many informations on the Internet.

答案：

...many pieces of information... (information 是不可數名詞)

或：...is much information...

5. He is a boy love basketball.

答案：

He is a boy who loves basketball. (句子缺動詞/連接詞，基礎句型錯誤)

6. (中譯英) 學生們應該要準時交作業。

答案：

Students should hand in their homework on time.

⚠️ 更多高頻易錯點 (陷阱題)

這些是學測中非常容易失分的「陷阱」，請特別留意：

1. One of my friends (live / lives) abroad.

答案：

...friends lives... (主詞是 "One"，不是 "friends")

2. The company bought new (equipments / equipment).

答案：

...new equipment. (equipment 是不可數名詞，沒有複數)

3. He gave me many good (advices / pieces of advice).

答案：

...good pieces of advice. (advice 也是不可數名詞)

4. The bad weather (effected / affected) our flight.

答案：

...affected (v. 影響) our flight. (effect 是名詞 "n. 影響；效果")

5. Each of the students (have / has) to join the club.

答案：

...students has... (主詞是 "Each"，是單數)

6. (中譯英) 住在城市裡有許多優點。

答案：

Living in the city has many advantages. (動名詞 "Living" 當主詞，視為單數)

或：There are many advantages to living in the city.

🔥 客製化練習 (Phase 1 基礎)

請找出並改正下列句子的錯誤：

1. There are many kind of park in the city.

答案：

...many kinds of parks... (many + 複數名詞)

2. The government build many facility for children.

答案：

...government builds many facilities... (S-V 一致 + 複數)

3. Children can do their favorite pastime in the park.

答案：

...favorite pastimes... (通常有多種)

4. The trees is planted everywhere in the park.

答案：

The trees are planted... (S-V 一致)

5. People can get close to the nature and feel relax.

答案：

...close to nature (不可數，不加 the) and feel relaxed (adj. 感到放鬆的).

6. The kids enjoy to play with their friends.

答案：

...enjoy playing... (enjoy + V-ing)

7. My parents brings me to park every weekend.

答案：

...parents bring me to the park... (S-V 一致 + 冠詞)

8. The vendor sell hotdog, ice cream and soda.

答案：

...vendor sells hotdogs... (S-V 一致 + 複數)

9. The birds is singing happily on the tree.

答案：

...birds are singing... in the tree.

10. There has a lake in the middle of the park.

答案：

There is a lake... (「有」的句型)

11. The park provide a space for people to release their stress.

答案：

...park provides... (S-V 一致)

12. This are the reason why people loves this park.

答案：

These are the reasons why people love... (This/These; S-V)

或：This is the reason why people love...

13. Everyone need a place where they can rest.

答案：

Everyone needs... (Everyone 為單數)

14. The parent sit under the tree and close eyes.

答案：

The parents sit... and close their eyes.

或：The parent sits... and closes his/her eyes.

15. The children make new friend and broaden their relationship of people.

答案：

...make new friends and broaden their relationships with people (或 social circle).

Phase 2：中階句型建構

目標：讓句子變得有密度、內容變扎實。

做到 Phase 2 → 作文穩定 16–18 分。

2-1：形容詞子句 (關係代名詞)

🎯 功能：補充說明 + 合併句子。這是學測最常提高句子層次的方式。

  • which → 代替物
  • who → 代替人
  • that → 泛用 (物/人都可)
  • where → 代替地方 (in which)

📘 模板 (句子合併)

原句：The park has a small lake. People like to walk around it.

✔ 升級：The park has a small lake which people like to walk around.

2-2：名詞子句

🎯 功能：讓內容抽象、提升層次。這是高分作文必出的句型。

  • that S + V (I believe that parks are important.)
  • what S + V (What people need is a park.)
  • why/how S + V (Why this is important is that...)

📘 模板 (一句變高分句)

原句：People need parks to relax.

✔ 升級：What people need is a park where they can truly relax.

Phase 2 練習任務（每日練習）

A. 拿你作文中的 3 句 → 用 which/that 重寫

B. 拿 2 句 → 用 名詞子句 重寫

（你可以把句子傳給我，我幫你檢查＋升級）

🎯 實戰演練與考試應用 (Phase 2)

🧠 考試怎麼用？(中階)

(which/who) 關代：用在「舉例」或「補充說明」時最好用。當你提到一個名詞 (a park, a friend, a method)，立刻用 "which..." 或 "who..." 接著補充說明，句子長度立刻加倍，內容也更扎實。

(that/what) 名詞子句：用在「開頭」或「表達觀點」時。與其說 "Parks are good."，不如說 "I believe that parks are good." 或 "What makes parks good is..."。後者分數立刻高一級。

中階句型練習：

1. (句子合併) The book is on the table. My teacher recommended it.

答案：

The book which (或 that) my teacher recommended is on the table.

2. (句子改寫) He said something. I don't understand it.

答案：

I don't understand what he said.

🔥 客製化練習 (Phase 2)

Phase 2-1：關係代名詞合併句練習

把兩句合成一句，用 which / that / who / where。

1. There is a lake in the park. People like to walk around the lake.

答案：

There is a lake in the park which (或 that) people like to walk around.

(進階：... a lake in the park around which people like to walk.)

2. The playground is full of children. They are laughing and shouting happily.

答案：

The playground is full of children who (或 that) are laughing and shouting happily.

3. I visited a park yesterday. The park is famous for its beautiful cherry blossoms.

答案：

I visited a park yesterday which (或 that) is famous for its beautiful cherry blossoms.

4. The government built a new park. The park provides many facilities for teenagers.

答案：

The government built a new park which (或 that) provides many facilities for teenagers.

5. I met a girl in the park. She loves drawing the scenery there.

答案：

I met a girl in the park who (或 that) loves drawing the scenery there.

6. There is a bench under the tree. Old people like to rest there in the afternoon.

答案：

There is a bench under the tree where old people like to rest in the afternoon.

7. I took some photos in the park. The photos remind me of my childhood.

答案：

I took some photos in the park which (或 that) remind me of my childhood.

8. The vendor sells hot dogs and ice cream. Many children line up to buy food from him.

答案：

The vendor, from whom many children line up to buy food, sells hot dogs and ice cream. (注意 "from whom" 與逗號)

9. The city has a park. The park is quiet and far from the traffic.

答案：

The city has a park which (或 that) is quiet and far from the traffic.

10. We found a small corner. We could sit there and listen to the sound of nature.

答案：

We found a small corner where we could sit and listen to the sound of nature.

Phase 2-2：名詞子句改寫練習

請用指定開頭改寫

1. Parks are important for people’s mental health. (用 I believe that...)

答案：

I believe that parks are important for people’s mental health.

2. We need a place where we can slow down. (用 What we need is...)

答案：

What we need is a place where we can slow down.

3. Many people feel stressed because they work too hard. (用 The reason is that...)

答案：

Many people feel stressed. The reason is that they work too hard.

4. People visit parks to relax, not just to exercise. (用 Why people visit parks is that...)

答案：

Why people visit parks is that they want to relax, not just to exercise.

5. A good park design can change people’s lives. (用 I am convinced that...)

答案：

I am convinced that a good park design can change people’s lives.

6. The park makes me feel calm and peaceful. (用 How the park makes me feel is...)

答案：

How the park makes me feel is calm and peaceful.

7. We should build more parks in big cities. (用 It is clear that...)

答案：

It is clear that we should build more parks in big cities.

8. The most important thing is that families can spend time together. (用 What matters most is that...)

答案：

What matters most is that families can spend time together.

9. I will never forget the day when I first visited this park. (用 I will never forget that... - 較不推薦)

答案：

I will never forget that day when I first visited this park. (此處 that 是形容詞 "那")

(原句的 "when..." 是形容詞子句，不是名詞子句)

10. Parks give people a chance to escape from the noise of the city. (用 That-clause 主詞...)

答案：

That parks give people a chance to escape from the noise of the city is undeniable.

Phase 3：進階句型掌握

目標：從普通變高分，文章出現「精緻句子」。

做到這裡 → 作文可達 18–19 分。

3-1：分詞構句 (Being/ V-ing / p.p.)

🎯 功能：讓文章看起來成熟、壓縮資訊。用來取代 "Because...", "When...", "While..."。

📘 模板

  • Being + adj., S + V (取代 Because S + is/was + adj.)
  • V-ing..., S + V (主動 / 同時發生)
  • p.p. ..., S + V (被動)

原句：Because people feel stressed, they visit parks to unwind.

✔ 升級：Feeling stressed, many people visit parks to unwind.

原句：The park is surrounded by trees, so it is very quiet.

✔ 升級：Surrounded by trees, the park is very quiet.

3-2：強調句 (It is ... that ...)

🎯 功能：強調你認為重要的東西 → 尤其適合「立論」或「結尾」。

原句：The peaceful environment makes the park special.

✔ 升級：It is the peaceful environment that makes the park special.

3-3：倒裝句 (Only / Not until)

🎯 功能：讓句子瞬間更「有文采」，適合用在結尾下結論。

原句：We can live a better life only when we protect nature.

✔ 升級：Only when we protect nature can we live a better life.

Phase 3 練習任務（每日練習）

1. 用「分詞構句」改寫 2 句 (取代 Because / When)

2. 用「It is … that」重寫 1 句 (強調你的論點)

3. 用 「Only when…」造 1 句 (當作結尾)

🎯 實戰演練與考試應用 (Phase 3)

🧠 考試怎麼用？(進階)

(分詞構句)：用在「描述背景」或「說明原因」時。不要整篇都用 "Because..."，偶爾用一句 "Feeling...", "Surrounded by..." 開頭，能讓教授眼睛一亮。

(It is...) 強調句：用在「第一段的論點句」或「第二段的開頭」。 "It is this reason that..."

(Only/Not until) 倒裝句：強烈建議只用在「文章結尾」。這是最強的結尾句型，例如："Only by doing so can we..." (唯有如此，我們才能...)。

進階句型練習：

1. (改寫) Because he is an honest man, I trust him.

答案：

Being an honest man, he is trusted by me. (主詞需一致)

或 (保留原主詞)：Believing he is an honest man, I trust him.

2. (改寫) We can achieve our dreams only by working hard.

答案：

Only by working hard can we achieve our dreams.

🔥 客製化練習 (Phase 3)

Phase 3-1：分詞構句改寫練習

把 because / when / while / 被動句，改成分詞構句開頭。

1. Because he felt tired, he sat down under the tree.

答案：

Feeling tired, he sat down under the tree.

2. When I walked into the park, I saw many children playing.

答案：

Walking into the park, I saw many children playing.

3. While they were listening to the birds, they forgot all their worries.

答案：

Listening to the birds, they forgot all their worries.

4. Because she was surrounded by nature, she felt peaceful.

答案：

Surrounded by nature, she felt peaceful.

5. When the sun was setting, the park became even more beautiful.

答案：

With the sun setting, the park became even more beautiful. (獨立分詞構句)

6. Because the park is located near my house, I go there very often.

答案：

Located near my house, the park is a place I go very often.

7. When we were walking along the path, we talked about our dreams.

答案：

Walking along the path, we talked about our dreams.

8. Because the parents were busy with work, they needed a place to relax.

答案：

Being busy with work, the parents needed a place to relax.

9. The park was designed by the government. It has many green areas.

答案：

Designed by the government, the park has many green areas.

10. The benches were placed under the trees. People could sit there comfortably.

答案：

Placed under the trees, the benches allowed people to sit comfortably.

(注意：主詞需一致。原句主詞 "People" 不同，直接改寫 "Placed..." 會變懸垂分詞。)

Phase 3-2：強調句 (It is...) 練習

指定「要強調的部分」，請改寫成 It is/was... that...

1. The peaceful atmosphere makes this park special. (強調 ...atmosphere)

答案：

It is the peaceful atmosphere that makes this park special.

2. I visit this park on weekends. (強調 on weekends)

答案：

It is on weekends that I visit this park.

3. My parents took me to this park for the first time. (強調 my parents)

答案：

It was my parents who (或 that) took me to this park for the first time.

4. The government built this park for children and the elderly. (強調 ...and the elderly)

答案：

It was for children and the elderly that the government built this park.

5. The trees along the path create a relaxing feeling. (強調 ...along the path)

答案：

It is the trees along the path that create a relaxing feeling.

6. I realized the value of nature during that summer. (強調 during that summer)

答案：

It was during that summer that I realized the value of nature.

7. This park helped me overcome my stress. (強調 this park)

答案：

It was this park that helped me overcome my stress.

8. I like to sit by the lake and think about my future. (強調 sit by the lake)

答案：

It is to sit by the lake that I like (most) and think about my future.

9. The laughter of children fills the whole park. (強調 ...of children)

答案：

It is the laughter of children that fills the whole park.

10. My ideal park brings joy to everyone in the city. (強調 ...to everyone in the city)

答案：

It is to everyone in the city that my ideal park brings joy.

Phase 3-3：倒裝句 (Only/Not until) 練習

用 Only when / Only by / Not until 改寫

1. We truly relax when we stay away from our phones. (用 Only when...)

答案：

Only when we stay away from our phones do we truly relax.

2. We can understand the value of nature when we lose it. (用 Not until...)

答案：

Not until we lose nature do we understand its value.

3. People can enjoy the beauty of the park only if they slow down. (用 Only if...)

答案：

Only if they slow down can people enjoy the beauty of the park.

4. I realized how tired I was when I sat on the bench. (用 Not until...)

答案：

Not until I sat on the bench did I realize how tired I was.

5. We can build a better city if we add more green spaces. (用 Only by...)

答案：

Only by adding more green spaces can we build a better city.

6. He found real peace after he started taking walks in the park. (用 Not until...)

答案：

Not until he started taking walks in the park did he find real peace.

7. I noticed the birds’ singing when I closed my eyes. (用 Only when...)

答案：

Only when I closed my eyes did I notice the birds’ singing.

8. People start to care about the environment after they see pollution. (用 Not until...)

答案：

Not until people see pollution do they start to care about the environment.

9. I felt grateful for this park when I was under great pressure. (用 Only when...)

答案：

Only when I was under great pressure did I feel grateful for this park.

10. We can protect nature if we work together. (用 Only by...)

答案：

Only by working together can we protect nature.

Phase 4：高階寫作技巧

目標：達到 19–20 分頂標水準。

做到這裡 → 你就是 20 分等級的寫手。

學習內容（高階）

4-1：主題句（Topic Sentence）

每段第一句都要明確說明這段在做什麼。

✔ 範例：

An ideal park should serve people of all ages.

4-2：段落內部連接 (Transition Signals)

使用轉折詞讓邏輯流暢：

  • To begin with, First of all...
  • Moreover, Furthermore, In addition...
  • Nevertheless, However...
  • As a result, Therefore, Consequently...

4-3：結尾呼應（首尾一致）

開頭談公園重要性 → 結尾用進階句型呼應：

✔ 範例：

Only by building better parks can we build a better city.

Phase 4 練習任務（段落組織）

依題目寫「兩段式文章」→ 我進行完整批改（依學測標準）。（此功能需在聊天中進行）

🎯 實戰演練與考試應用 (Phase 4)

🧠 考試怎麼用？(高階)

這是「邏輯」與「架構」層級。您的句型 (Phase 1-3) 就像磚塊，Phase 4 則是教您如何蓋房子。

在考場上，第一段的「第一句」和第二段的「第一句」 (Topic Sentence) 決定了您 70% 的分數。寫完主題句後，請用轉折詞 (Moreover, However, As a result) 確保您的論點是「向前推進」，而不是「原地打轉」。

段落建構練習：

請試著用轉折詞，將以下三個分散的論點，組合成一段有邏輯的段落 (關於「社群媒體的壞處」)：

  • (主題句) Using social media too much can be harmful.
  • (論點1) It wastes a lot of our valuable time.
  • (論點2) It makes people feel anxious about their lives (due to comparison).
  • (結論) We should use it more wisely.

範例答案：

Using social media too much can be harmful. To begin with, it wastes a lot of our valuable time which could be spent on more meaningful activities. Moreover, it often makes people feel anxious about their own lives when they see others' seemingly perfect posts. As a result, it is crucial that we learn to use it more wisely.
