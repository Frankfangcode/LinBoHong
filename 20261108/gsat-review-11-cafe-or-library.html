04

詳細錯誤訂正

答案目前蓋住中。先對照每一條的錯誤代碼自己改寫，再按上方按鈕掀開。

PARAGRAPH 1

01we can see a women is studies through her ipad in cafePLCHPREP
➡️we can see a woman studying on her iPad in a café

一個人要用單數 woman。see + 人 + V-ing 表示「看到某人正在做某事」，is studies 是兩個動詞疊在一起。用平板讀書的介系詞是 on。iPad 是品牌名，寫法固定。café 是可數名詞，前面要加 a。

02she is taking a photo by her phonePREP
➡️she is taking a photo with her phone

用工具做事用 with。by 接交通方式，例如 by bus。

03There are nothing on the table instead books.SVWW
➡️There is nothing on the table except books.

nothing 是單數，要用 There is。instead 是「代替」，「除了…以外」要用 except 或 but。

04There are both advantages and disadvantages to the place where they study.PL
➡️Both places have their own advantages and disadvantages.

講的是兩個地方，place 要用複數。改成 Both places 當主詞，句子比較短也比較清楚。

05First, studies in the cafe can enjoy the atmosphere around youCHREF
➡️First, when studying in a café, you can enjoy the relaxing atmosphere

studies（讀書）不能當「享受」的主詞，要讓 you 去享受。後面又用了 around you，前後主詞要一致。

06and eat some snacks after studyWW
➡️and have some snacks after studying

after 是介系詞，後面接 V-ing。圖 A 桌上有一塊蛋糕，可以直接寫進去當細節。

07Second, studying in a cafe can be distractingLOG
➡️However, studying in a café can be distracting

First 講好處，接著講壞處，用 Second 會讓人以為是第二個好處。轉到缺點要用 However。

08due to the background noise, and buying drinks noiseCH
➡️because of the background music and the noise of people ordering drinks

buying drinks noise 是中文「買飲料的聲音」直譯，英文要說 the noise of people ordering drinks。

09the main advantage… is a quiet environment and the feeling, which help students stay focusedREF
➡️the main advantage… is its quiet and serious atmosphere, which helps students stay focused

the feeling 沒說是什麼感覺，讀者不知道指什麼。要寫清楚是「認真讀書的氛圍」。

PARAGRAPH 2

10As for my personal choiseSP
➡️As for my personal choice

choice 拼錯。

11without unnecessary distractionPL
➡️without unnecessary distractions

「讓人分心的事物」是可數名詞，這裡泛指要用複數。

12keeps me motivated, which upon my learning efficiencyMISS
➡️keeps me motivated, which boosts my learning efficiency

which 子句少了動詞。「提升效率」用 boosts、improves 或 increases。

05

高分優化建議

切題度：第二段的理由要展開

  • 第一段完全切題，兩張圖、各自的好處和壞處都有寫到。
  • 第二段太短，兩個理由各只有一句話，沒有例子，所以內容分卡在中段。每個理由後面補一句親身經驗，例如「我在咖啡廳讀書時，常常被隔壁的聊天聲打斷，一小時只讀完幾頁」，就具體多了。
  • 可以加一句讓步，例如「雖然咖啡廳比較舒服，但對我來說，專心比舒服重要」。這樣能回扣第一段，讓兩段連起來。

組織邏輯：第二段的骨架

  • 第一段：圖 A 的好處 → 壞處，接著 On the other hand → 圖 B 的好處 → 壞處。只要把 Second 改成 However，結構就很清楚。
  • 第二段：主題句 → 理由一＋例子 → 理由二＋例子 → 讓步 → 結尾句。這篇只有前兩個部分，後面都缺。

06

滿分示範改寫

理由還是原本的兩個：安靜不分心、看到別人努力有動力。綠底句是第二段新增的例子、讓步和結尾句。

The two pictures show two different places for learning. In Picture A, a woman is studying on her iPad in a café while taking a photo of her cake with her phone. In Picture B, two students are reading quietly in a library, with nothing on their tables except books. Both places have their own advantages and disadvantages. When studying in a café, people can enjoy the cozy atmosphere and have some snacks when they need a break. However, a café can also be distracting because of the background music and the noise of people ordering drinks. On the other hand, the main advantage of studying in a library is its quiet and serious atmosphere, which helps students stay focused. Still, the strict silence can sometimes feel overwhelming and rigid.

As for my personal choice, I prefer studying in the library. The main reason is that the quiet environment helps me focus without unnecessary distractions. Whenever I study in a café, the chatter from nearby tables often interrupts my thoughts, and I can only finish a few pages in an hour. In the library, by contrast, I can concentrate for a long time without looking at my phone. Besides, seeing other people working hard keeps me motivated, which boosts my learning efficiency. Although a café may be more comfortable, staying focused matters more to me. Therefore, the library is the best place for me to study.

約 230 words ｜ 第二段：主題句 → 理由一＋例子 → 理由二 → 讓步 → 結尾
