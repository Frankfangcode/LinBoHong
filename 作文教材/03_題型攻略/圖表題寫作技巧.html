圖表題寫作技巧

學習如何有條理地呈現事實和數據，分析事理，並透過引用證據來表達意見，加強文章說服力。

第一式：長條圓餅說分明

圖表能將數據視覺化，透過圖像（diagram/graph）與表格（table/chart）達到不同功能，如呈現趨勢或分布、相互比較、視覺化組成、顯示關係等等。本節將學習解讀圖表與比較數據的技巧。

B. 描述趨勢的必備詞彙

掌握描述數字、比例、種類和趨勢變化的關鍵字詞，是圖表寫作的基礎。

① 數字 (number)

  • figure: 數字
  • amount: 數量 (不可數)
  • data: 資料、數據
  • statistics: 統計數據
  • sum: 總額

The figures/numbers in the report need to be read critically. (要謹慎閱讀報告裡的數字。)

The number of private colleges has increased. = Private colleges have increased in number. (私立大學數目增加。)

② 比例 (proportion)

  • percentage: 百分比
  • proportion: 比率、比例
  • rate: 速率、比率
  • share: 份額

The literacy rate rose to 85 percent. (識字率成長到 85%。)

A large percentage of schoolbooks now have e-book versions. (現在很高比例的學校用書有電子版。)

The proportion of men to women at my college is about three to one. (我們大學的男女比例是三比一。)

③ 種類 (kind)

  • kind: 種類
  • type: 類型
  • category: 類別

The chart shows the time spent by high school students in Taiwan on different kinds of extracurricular activities. (此圖呈現台灣高中生在不同課外活動所花的時間。)

These studies can be divided into three categories. (這個研究可以分為三類。)

④ 顯示 (show)

  • show: 顯示
  • illustrate: 說明
  • suggest: 表明
  • present: 呈現
  • indicate: 顯示
  • depict: 描述
  • represent: 代表

This graph demonstrates the different stages of our project over the past three years. (這張圖呈現我們的計畫在過去三年以來的不同階段。)

UNESCO figures show two-thirds of an academic year are lost on average worldwide due to Covid-19-related school closures. (聯合國教科文組織的數字顯示，新冠疫情導致的停課已造成全球平均損失達三分之二學年。)

⑦ 表示狀態維持

  • 動詞: remain, stay
  • 形容詞: constant, stable, steady, unchanged
  • 動詞片語: to flatten out, to hold steady, to level off, to stabilize

We are expecting a slow but steady economic recovery. (我們期待經濟緩慢而穩定的復甦。)

C. 說明倍數關係的句型

要更精準地比較數據，倍數句型是不可或缺的工具。

  1. S₁ + be/V₁ + twice / three times as adj/adv as S₂

    Kelly earns three times as much as I do. (凱莉賺的錢是我的三倍。)

  2. S₁ + be/V₁ + two / three times comparative adj/adv than S₂

    We have produced four times more milk than we did last year. (我們生產了比去年多四倍的牛乳。)

  3. S₁ + be/V₁ + half / twice / three times the noun of S₂

    This lightweight ceiling is half the weight of a conventional concrete ceiling. (這種輕型天花板的重量是傳統水泥天花板的一半。)

詮釋圖表三步驟 (進階篇)

掌握了基本詞彙與句型後，我們可以用三個步驟來快速掌握並詮釋圖表重點。

  1. 用標題圖說點出主軸：開門見山，說明圖表類型與主題。
  2. 挑選顯著的數據為主體：找出最大值、最小值或最突出的類別。
  3. 運用倍數比較詮釋數據：使用倍數句型強化對比，深入分析。

範例：1990 年代全球墮胎數量

Step 1: 點出主軸
The two graphs depict the occurrence of abortions in developed and developing nations in the 1990s. One illustrates the occurrence of legal and safe abortions, and the other, the occurrence of illegal and unsafe abortions.
這兩張圖呈現已開發與開發中國家在 1990 年代的人工流產的數量，一張表示合法且安全的人工流產數量，另一張則是呈現非法且不安全的人工流產數量。

Step 2: 挑選顯著數據
The graph shows that more than half of all legal abortions took place in developing countries.
這張圖顯示，在所有合法人工流產數量中，發展中國家佔了一半以上。

Step 3: 運用倍數比較 (見下方範文)

整合段落範例：

The two graphs here depict the occurrence of abortions in developed and developing nations in the 1990s. As illustrated in the bar chart on the left, the number of legal and safe abortions in developing countries was slightly larger than that in developed countries. Nevertheless, we see a considerable difference in the bar chart on the right. The number of illegal or unsafe abortions in developing countries was nine times as large as that in developed countries. The significant differences between the two suggest that women's healthcare in developing countries needs improvement.

第二式：比較對比 (夾心餅乾升級版)

在描述完圖表後，大考作文常要求我們結合自身經驗，進行比較與對比。這時就需要運用升級版的「OREO 夾心餅乾」架構來組織觀點。

A. 比較與對比的常用語

比較 (尋找相似處) 對比 (尋找差異處)
like, similar to, similarly, the same as, just as, both, as well as, also, too, likewise, have something in common unlike, different from, compared to, despite, in spite of, although, but, yet, while, on the contrary, on the other hand, however, nevertheless, otherwise

B. 夾心餅乾 OREO 英文用語

OREO 是一個簡單有效的論述架構：提出觀點 (Opinion)、說明理由 (Reason)、提供舉例 (Example)，最後重申觀點 (Opinion)。

  • Opinion (觀點)

    In my opinion, I think, I believe, It seems to me that...

  • Reason (原因)

    because, since, due to, therefore, as a result, consequently...

  • Example (舉例)

    for instance, for example, while, whereas, some... others..., but, however...

  • Opinion (重申觀點)

    in conclusion, in short, to sum up, therefore, altogether, finally...

[E] 舉例: For example, I am a member of the school's basketball team, and we practice for at least two hours every day after school. This is quite different from the American students, who spend less than an hour on sports.
[O] 觀點: In my view, my daily schedule is quite different from that of the American high school students shown in the chart, primarily due to different academic pressures.
[O] 重申觀點: In conclusion, while we both spend a significant amount of time on academics, the allocation for other activities like sports and part-time jobs highlights the different priorities in our high school lives.
[R] 理由: This is mainly because in Taiwan, students face immense pressure to get into a good university, so we tend to spend more time on studying and attending cram schools, leaving less time for part-time jobs or leisure.

108年指考作文練習

提示：右表顯示美國18至29歲的青年對不同類別之新聞的關注度統計。請依據圖表內容寫一篇英文作文，文長至少120個單詞。第一段描述圖表內容，並指出關注度較高及偏低的類別；第二段則描述在這六個新聞類別中，你自己較為關注及較不關注的新聞主題分別為何，並說明理由。

寫作步驟提示
  1. 用標題圖示點出主軸：點出圖表標題後，可由關注度高至低依序描述各類別。
  2. 挑選顯著的數據為主體：指出關注度最高（環境與天然災害）與最低（藝術與文化）的新聞議題。
  3. 運用倍數比較詮釋數據：以倍數或對比突顯項目間的差異，詮釋數據。
  4. 比較與對比：拿自己和圖表呈現的訊息相比，有哪些相似處與相異處？描述自己關注的新聞類型，哪個最多？哪個最少？記得說明原因，提供例子與細節。

結論技巧進階

一個好的結論能讓文章餘韻無窮。除了重申論點，還可以嘗試以下方式：

  • 重溫論點 (Reminding): 簡單扼要地重新申明自己的論點。
    常用語：as stated above, for these reasons
  • 凸顯關鍵 (Emphasizing): 再次強調最重要的字詞。
    常用語：most importantly, in fact, after all
  • 創造餘韻 (Resonating): 訴諸情感、拋出問題、做出呼籲或展望未來。
    常用語：I hope..., What if...?, We should..., In the future,...

範例 (109 年指考題：校園安全):

第一段 (O-R-E)
In my opinion, a safe campus environment is a priority... Keeping schools safe allows students to learn... However, campus safety has become a growing concern... In fact, violent acts in schools occur at all educational levels... I personally have a close friend who had long been bullied...

第二段 (R-E-O)
Different measures and precautions can be taken... For instance, we students should be responsible for our words and behavior... As stated above, school safety is a vital issue. [重申論點] Will we ever realize how important it is to maintain a safe and nurturing learning environment? [拋出問題] School staff and students alike should play their parts... [做出呼籲] After all, prevention is better than cure. [凸顯關鍵] With all the right measures taken, we will eventually benefit from the efforts we make. [展望未來]
