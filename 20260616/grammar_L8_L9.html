L8-1 Only 的倒裝句
⭐ 核心判斷：什麼時候才要倒裝？

Only 引導副詞修飾語（介系詞片語、副詞子句、時間副詞）放在句首時，主句動詞需要倒裝。

✓ Only when / after / if … 子句 ✓ Only + 介系詞片語（by/in/at…） ✓ Only then / only at that moment ✗ Only + 主詞 → 不倒裝
記憶方法：問自己「only 後面的東西是『修飾語』還是『主詞』？」
只有 my boyfriend / Grandma / the teacher 這種接人或名詞當主詞的，才不倒裝。

三種動詞類型的倒裝方式

① 一般動詞 — 用 do/does/did 幫忙
Only [副詞語] + do/does/did + S + 原V
You realize its value only when you lose your health.
→ Only when you lose your health do you realize its value.
時態跟原句一樣：原句用現在式 → does/do；過去式 → did，後面動詞還原成原形。
② Be 動詞 — Be 直接移到主詞前
Only [副詞語] + be + S + …
Citizens of Taiwan are allowed to vote only after they turn 20.
→ Only after they turn 20 are citizens of Taiwan allowed to vote.
③ 自帶助動詞（can / will / must / should…）— 助動詞移到主詞前
Only [副詞語] + 助動詞 + S + 原V
We can succeed only by working hard.
→ Only by working hard can we succeed.
I will not stop saying "sorry" until you accept my apology.
→ Not until you accept my apology will I stop saying "sorry".

完整對照表

類型 原句 倒裝句
一般動詞
do/does/did		 You realize its value only when you lose your health. Only when you lose your health do you realize its value.
Be 動詞 Citizens are allowed to vote only after they turn 20. Only after they turn 20 are citizens allowed to vote.
助動詞 We can succeed only by working hard. Only by working hard can we succeed.
Not until
否定字倒裝		 I didn't do homework until mom came home. Not until mom came home did I do homework.
❌ 這些句子 Only 放句首但「不倒裝」
Only my boyfriend can kiss me. → boyfriend 是主詞 Only Grandma has that apple pie recipe. → Grandma 是主詞

Only 後面直接接主詞（人名/代名詞/名詞片語當主詞），句子正常語序，不需倒裝。

L8-2 倍數表達法
⭐ 核心規則：倍數加在比較結構前面

只要把「倍數詞」加在比較級前面就好，有兩種句型，意思完全一樣。

N times + as 原級 as
Tom is three times as tall as Peter.
N times + 比較級 + than
Tom is three times taller than Peter.
注意：as … as 中間一定放原級（不能加 -er！）
倍數怎麼說
倍數說法備注
1 倍❌ 沒有「once」這種用法
2 倍twice特殊字，不用 two times
3 倍three times數字 + times
4 倍以上four / five / ten … times數字 + times
⭐ 不可數用 much，可數用 many
不可數名詞 → much
He has three times as much money as I do.
（錢、時間、水→不可數）
可數名詞 → many
She has twice as many books as he does.
（書、蘋果、人→可數）

形容詞 vs 副詞對照

類型as…as 句型比較級 + than 句型
形容詞
Tom 是 Peter 的 3 倍高		 Tom is three times as tall as Peter. Tom is three times taller than Peter.
副詞
獵豹跑得比人快 2 倍		 Cheetahs run twice as fast as humans. Cheetahs run twice faster than humans.
動詞（花費）
進口蘋果是國內的 3 倍貴		 Imported apples cost three times as much as domestic ones. Imported apples cost three times more than domestic ones.
L9-1 助動詞—對未知情況的推測/猜想
推測強弱程度（由強→弱）
must
一定
may
可能
might
可能
could
可能

must 確定度最高（幾乎肯定），could 最低（有可能，但不太確定）。四個字都是「正面推測」（覺得可能發生）。

① 對現在 / 未來事件的推測
S + must/may/might/could + 原V
Ellen is sleeping now.
→ She must be very tired.
The student raised his hand.
→ He might have a question to ask.
現在推測：助動詞 + 原形動詞（be / have / go…）
② 對過去事件的推測
S + must/may/might/could + have + p.p.
The house was a total mess.
→ Someone must have broken in.
I can't find my cell phone.
→ I could have lost it somewhere.
過去推測：助動詞 + have + 過去分詞 (p.p.)
⭐ 最常考的判斷：現在 vs 過去 vs 進行
狀況句型例句
推測現在狀態 must/may + 原V It's midnight, Tom is still studying. There must be an important test tomorrow.
推測現在進行中 must/may + be + V-ing Tina has been practicing day and night. She must be preparing for a contest.
推測過去發生 must/may + have + p.p. I called many times, no one answered. They must have gone on a vacation.
⚠️ 跟假設語氣的助動詞要分開！
假設語氣（與現實相反）
If I were you, I would study harder.
→ 語氣助動詞：would/could/should/might
推測用法（猜測事實）
She must be tired. / He might have left.
→ 推測助動詞：must/may/might/could
填空 牛刀小試 — 填寫後對答案

L8-1 Only 倒裝句改寫

1. We can only see this kind of insect in the Amazon rainforest.
→ Only in the Amazon rainforest this kind of insect.
2. The castle is only accessible by boat.
→ Only by boat .
3. The wife will go to bed only when the husband comes home.
→ Only when the husband comes home .
4. Josh reunites with his family only on New Year's Eve.
→ Only with his family.
5. Those kids only behave themselves at home.
→ Only themselves.
6. I am happy only when my girlfriend is with me.
→ Only when my girlfriend is with me .

L8-2 倍數表達填空

1. John 比 Josh 常（often）健身 2 倍。
→ John works out as as Josh.
= John works out than Josh.
2. 進口蘋果是國內蘋果要價的三倍。
→ Imported apples cost three as as domestic ones.
= Imported apples cost three than domestic ones.
3. 這棟摩天大樓是那棟建築物的十倍高。
→ This skyscraper that building.
= This skyscraper that building.
4. 男人重 100 公斤，太太重 50 公斤。用 as...as 合併。
→ The man weighs his wife.
= The man is his wife.
5. 爺爺 90 歲，弟弟 9 歲。（10 倍）
→ My grandfather is my younger brother.
→ My grandfather is my younger brother.
6. 國中學 2000 字，高中學 8000 字（4 倍）
→ I will learn times in senior high school than I in junior high school.
