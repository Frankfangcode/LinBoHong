Chapter 12

形容詞 (Adjective)

⭐ 形容詞的四種位置
位置說明例句
名詞前修飾後面的名詞She is a friendly lady.
名詞後（後位修飾）修飾不定代名詞（somebody/anyone…）或接子句/片語Did you see anything strange?
be / 連綴動詞後作主詞補語，修飾主詞The movie is exciting.
受詞後作受詞補語（S+V+O+OC）The noise drove me crazy.
⚠️ 後位修飾必考：somebody / anyone / nothing / everything… 等不定代名詞，形容詞必須放後面！
I need someone responsible. ✓    responsible someone ✗
以 a- 開頭的形容詞 — 只能放 be 動詞後，不能放名詞前！

Chapter 12

比較句型

⭐ 原級比較
句型一：S + V + as + adj./adv. + as... （和……一樣）
句型二：S + V + not + as/so + adj./adv. + as... （不如……）
句型三：S + V + as + much/many + N + as... （和……一樣多）
句型四：S + V + as + adj. + as + possible / one can（盡可能……）
Amy is as beautiful as Alison.  |  She cooks as well as my mom.  |  Run as fast as possible.
⭐ 比較級句型（7大句型）
① S + be + adj.-er / more adj. + than... （比……更……）
② S + be + the + 比較級 + of the two（兩者中較……的）
③ Adj.-er and adj.-er / more and more + adj.（越來越……）
④ The + 比較級..., the + 比較級...（越……越……）
⑤ superior / inferior / senior / junior + to（不用 than！）
⑥ S + had no sooner + p.p. ... than + 過去式（一……就……）
⑦ S + know better + than to V（不會笨到去做……）
⚠️ 比較級前可加 much / far / even / still / a lot 加強；絕對不能加 very！
⭐ 最高級句型
S + V + the + adj.-est / most adj. + among/of + 範圍（在……中最……）
by far the + 最高級（強調程度最強的說法）
⚠️ 比較級表最高級：heavier than any other + 單數N  |  No other student is as heavy as Piggy.
形容詞比較級構成（規則）
類型比較級最高級
一般（tall）taller(the) tallest
字尾 -e（cute）cuter(the) cutest
短母+子音（big）bigger(the) biggest
子音+y（happy）happier(the) happiest
多音節（beautiful）more beautiful(the) most beautiful
不規則變化
原級比較級最高級
good / wellbetter(the) best
bad / illworse(the) worst
littleless(the) least
many / muchmore(the) most
oldolder / elderoldest / eldest
farfarther / furtherfarthest / furthest
L8-1

Only 的倒裝句

⭐ 核心規則：什麼情況倒裝？

Only 引導副詞修飾語（介系詞片語、副詞子句、時間副詞）放在句首時，主句動詞需要倒裝。

✓ Only when/after/if… 子句 ✓ Only + 介系詞片語（by/in/at…） ✓ Only then / at that moment ✗ Only + 主詞 → 不倒裝！
Only my boyfriend can kiss me. → boyfriend 是主詞，不倒裝！
三種動詞的倒裝方式
動詞類型倒裝方式例句
一般動詞do / does / did + S + 原VOnly when you lose health do you realize its value.
Be 動詞be + SOnly after they turn 20 are citizens allowed to vote.
助動詞助動詞 + S + 原VOnly by working hard can we succeed.
Not until同一般動詞規則Not until mom came home did I do homework.
⚠️ Only when 子句本身不倒裝！Only when 後面的子句語序正常，只有主句才倒裝。
Only when you lose health do you realize... ← 子句正常，主句倒裝
L8-2

倍數表達法

⭐ 兩大句型 — 意思完全相同
N times + as + 原級 + as
Tom is three times as tall as Peter.
N times + 比較級 + than
Tom is three times taller than Peter.
⚠️ as...as 中間放原級（絕對不能 -er！）
倍數說法
倍數說法
2 倍twice（特殊字，不用 two times）
3 倍以上three / four / ten... times
可數 vs 不可數
He has twice as much money as I do.
（money 不可數 → much）
She has twice as many books as he does.
（books 可數 → many）
倍數 + 名詞句型（Adj.→N 轉換）
形容詞對應名詞例
oldageHe's twice my age. ✓   twice the age of me ✗
tall / highheightfive times the height of
expensivepricefour times the price of
heavyweightthree times the weight of
L9-1

助動詞 — 對未知情況的推測

推測強弱程度（由強→弱）
must
一定（最確定）
may
可能
might
可能（略低）
could
可能（最低）
① 推測現在 / 未來
S + must/may/might/could + 原V
Tom is still studying. There must be an important test tomorrow.
（推測現在/未來的事）
② 推測現在進行
S + must/may/might + be + V-ing
Tina practices day and night. She must be preparing for a contest.
（推測現在正在進行的動作）
③ 推測過去 — 最常考！
S + must/may/might/could + have + p.p.
I called many times, no one answered. They must have gone on vacation.
The house is a mess. Someone must have broken in last night.
⚠️ 判斷現在 vs 過去 vs 進行的關鍵：看線索！
• 發現了結果/痕跡（過去）→ must have + p.p.
• 現在還在做（進行）→ must be + V-ing
• 推測現在或未來的狀態 → must + 原V
L7-1

假設語氣延伸 — 要不是…

⭐ 兩大句型對照
與現在事實相反與過去事實相反
If 句型If it were not for + N,
S + would/could/should/might + 原V		If it had not been for + N,
S + would/could/should/might + have p.p.
= But forBut for + N, S + would + 原VBut for + N, S + would + have p.p.
= WithoutWithout + N, S + would + 原VWithout + N, S + would + have p.p.
倒裝句Were it not for + N, ...Had it not been for + N, ...
現在相反 — 例句
If it were not for your help, I would fail.
= But for your help, I would fail.
= Without your help, I would fail.
= Were it not for your help, I would fail.
過去相反 — 例句
If it had not been for your help, I would have failed.
= But for your help, I would have failed.
= Without your help, I would have failed.
= Had it not been for your help, I would have failed.
⚠️ 倒裝方式：砍掉 If，把 were / had 移到主詞前面。
現在：If it were not for → Were it not for
過去：If it had not been for → Had it not been for
複習：基本假設語氣
類型If 子句主句
現在相反If + S + were/V-edS + would/could + 原V
過去相反If + S + had + p.p.S + would/could + have p.p.
