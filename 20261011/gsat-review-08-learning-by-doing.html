詳細錯誤訂正

PARAGRAPH 1

01The three pictures illustrate an activity is "Learning by Doing".CHPL
➡️The three pictures illustrate three activities that embody "learning by doing."

一個子句裡有 illustrate 和 is 兩個動詞，要用關係代名詞 that 連接。三張圖畫的是三個活動，所以用複數。

02In the picture AART
➡️In Picture A

「Picture + 編號」視為專有名稱，不加 the。

03four students have an experience to be a volunteerWWPL
➡️four students are volunteering at an animal shelter

have an experience to be 是中文「有當志工的經驗」直譯。主詞是四個人，volunteer 也要複數，直接用 volunteering 最自然。

04They have all different kinds job.MISSPL
➡️They are doing different kinds of jobs.

kinds 後面要接 of，而且 job 要用複數。

05a girl is swiping the house of the dogsSPWW
➡️a girl is sweeping out the dog kennel

swipe 是「滑（手機）、刷（卡）」，掃地是 sweep。狗屋是 kennel 或 doghouse，不說 the house of the dogs。

06some dogs feeded by a girlSP
➡️some dogs fed by a girl

feed 是不規則動詞，三態是 feed–fed–fed。

07…fed by a girl and a boy walking a black dog.CH
➡️…fed by a girl. Next to her, a boy is walking a black dog.

原句會被讀成「狗被一個女孩和一個遛狗的男孩餵」，語意不清，應拆成兩句。

08there have a boy cleaning the floorCH
➡️there is a boy cleaning the floor

「有」在英文要用 there is/are，不能用 there have。

09the teacher in teaching the studentMISSPL
➡️the teacher is teaching the students

少了 be 動詞 is（in 應該是筆誤），學生不只一位，要用複數。

10They are mocking trail.WWSP
➡️They are holding a mock trial.

全篇最嚴重的錯誤。mock 當動詞是「嘲笑」，trail 是「小徑」，原句變成「他們在嘲笑小路」。模擬法庭是 a mock trial，這裡 mock 是形容詞。

11through the class. Students can easily learning how the trail working.FRAGPT
➡️Through this class, students can easily learn how a trial works.

through the class 單獨成句是不完整句，句首也沒大寫。can 後面接原形動詞。how 引導的間接問句用直述語序：主詞＋動詞（a trial works）。

12All students seperate in different groups.SPWW
➡️The students are divided into several groups.

separate 拼錯（中間是 -par-）。「被分組」用被動語態 be divided into 最自然。

PARAGRAPH 2

13From my perspective. I extremely want to enjoy the stray animal shelter class.PFRAGWW
➡️From my perspective, I would most like to join the animal shelter volunteering activity.

From my perspective 後面用逗號，不能用句號。extremely 不能修飾 want，要說 really want 或 would most like to。上課用 join 或 take，不用 enjoy。

14because there are many cats and dogs were lose on the roadCHSP
➡️because many cats and dogs are abandoned on the streets

there are 和 were 兩個動詞重複。lose 是動詞「遺失」，形容詞是 lost。流浪動物多半是「被棄養」，用 abandoned 更精準。

15don't have a safety homeWW
➡️do not have a safe home

safety 是名詞「安全」，修飾名詞要用形容詞 safe。正式作文不用縮寫。

16So that, I want to…WW
➡️Therefore, I want to…

so that 是連接詞，表示「以便」，不能放句首加逗號當「所以」用。

17have an experience to take good care of the petsWW
➡️gain experience in taking care of the animals

「累積經驗」的固定搭配是 gain experience in + V-ing。收容所的動物還沒有主人，用 animals 比 pets 精準。

18the volunteer needs a kind heart to have a job and I have tooREFMISS
➡️a volunteer needs a kind heart to do this job well, and I happen to have one

to have a job 語意怪，應是「把這工作做好」。I have too 少了受詞，要說 I have one。

19the school can make have these kind of the classCHPLART
➡️schools should offer more classes of this kind

make 和 have 兩個動詞連用不合文法。these kind 單複數不一致（this kind／these kinds）。of 後面的 the class 不需要 the。

20Let more students can have a chance to know…FRAG
➡️…so that more students can discover that…

Let 開頭變成祈使句，而且 let + 受詞後面接原形動詞，不能加 can。這裡用 so that 接到上一句最順。

21is a good chace of learningSPWW
➡️is a meaningful way to learn

chance 拼錯。做中學是一種「方式」，不是一個「機會」。

05

高分優化建議

切題度：第二段要回扣「做中學」

  • 題目核心是 learning by doing，但兩個理由都在講「動物很可憐」和「我很善良」，沒說明親身去做能學到什麼。高分作文會寫出具體收穫：責任感、耐心、照顧動物的實際技能、課本教不到的東西。
  • I have a kind heart 只是自我宣稱，說服力弱。改成「我想把善心化為行動」或「我想學會和不會說話的動物溝通」會好很多。
  • 結論變成呼籲學校多開課，可以當收尾，但要先把「我」的收穫講清楚再帶過去。

組織邏輯：字數要分給第二段

  • 第一段描述約 120 字，第二段論述只有約 90 字。分數多半在第二段，第一段每張圖精簡成 1–2 句就夠。
  • 第二段先用一句主題句點出選擇（Of the three activities, I would most like to…），再用 To begin with / Moreover / All in all 帶出理由。

06

滿分示範改寫

保留原本的想法：三張圖的描述、選收容所、流浪動物沒有家、想學會照顧牠們、需要善心、學校應多開這類課。

The three pictures show three different activities that put the idea of "learning by doing" into practice. In Picture A, four students are volunteering at an animal shelter, each with a different task. A girl on the left is sweeping out a dog kennel, while another girl is feeding a group of hungry dogs. Next to her, a boy is walking a black dog, and behind them, another boy is mopping the floor. In Picture B, students are acting out a mock trial under their teacher's guidance. By playing different roles in a courtroom, they can easily understand how a real trial works. In Picture C, students are taking a robot assembly class. Divided into small groups, they are discussing how to put their robots together.

Of the three activities, I would most like to volunteer at the animal shelter. To begin with, countless cats and dogs are abandoned on the streets every year, and many of them never find a safe home. Instead of merely feeling sorry for them, I want to roll up my sleeves and learn how to take proper care of these animals—how to feed them, clean their living space, and win their trust. Moreover, I believe that working with animals requires patience and a kind heart, two qualities I have always valued. Through hands-on experience at the shelter, I could turn my kindness into real action and learn responsibility in a way no textbook can teach. All in all, I firmly believe that schools should offer more activities like these, so that more students can discover that learning by doing is one of the most meaningful ways to learn.
