住宿舍還是租房子
批改與檢討

林柏宏・英文作文。第一段選擇住宿舍或在外租屋並說明原因；第二段討論這個選擇可能面對的挑戰，以及如何解決。文長至少 120 words。

作文預估級分
9/20
落點 8–10 分

一句話總評：第一段有選擇、有三個理由，第二段也用了自己的夏令營住宿經驗，素材很真實；但第二段完全沒寫「怎麼解決」，題目的要求少了一半，再加上句構錯誤很密集，內容分被壓得很低。

扣分主因排序：① 第二段只寫挑戰、沒寫解決方法，題目要求少一半。② 立場放在第一段最後一句，還先替「在外租屋」講話，立場模糊。③ 少 be 動詞、make me can't、can makes 這類基本句構錯誤。

02

題目

題本原文

上大學後，許多人面臨著住在學校宿舍（live in the school dorm）或是自己租屋（rent an apartment）的選擇。請同學寫一篇兩段的文章：

  1. 第一段討論在上述兩者中，你會挑選哪一個，並說明原因。
  2. 第二段請討論你會因為這樣的選擇而可能面對到的挑戰以及可以如何解決。

這次漏掉的：第二段的「可以如何解決」。

04

詳細錯誤訂正

答案目前蓋住中。先對照每一條的錯誤代碼自己改寫，再按上方按鈕掀開。

PARAGRAPH 1

01many people needs to make a choice for live in the school dorm or rent an apartmentSVPREP
➡️many people need to choose between living in a school dorm and renting an apartment

people 是複數，動詞不加 s。「在 A 和 B 之間選擇」用 choose between A and B，介系詞後面的動詞要改成 V-ing。

02the school dorm would cheaper than the house you rent outsideMISS
➡️living in a school dorm is cheaper than renting an apartment

cheaper 是形容詞，前面少了 be 動詞。比較的對象要對等：「住宿舍」對「租房子」。

03save more money for your entertainments, traffic or even prepare for futurePLWWART
➡️save money for entertainment and transportation, or even for the future

entertainment 是不可數名詞，不加 s。traffic 是「路上的車流」，交通費要用 transportation。prepare 和前面的名詞不對等，直接改成 for the future，future 前面要加 the。

04it might be safer than rent an apartmentWW
➡️it might be safer than renting an apartment

than 後面的比較對象要和主詞對等，用 V-ing。

05You can face the problems with your roommates.LOG
➡️If something goes wrong, you have roommates to help you.

這句沒有說明「為什麼比較安全」。要把安全和室友的關係講清楚，例如有人照應、有宿舍管理員。

06the distance in school dorm may closer than your apartmentMISSCH
➡️the dorm is usually closer to your classrooms than an off-campus apartment

同樣少了 be 動詞。「距離比較近」英文說 A is closer to B，不說 the distance in A is closer。

07You can slow down your step to look around the school.WW
➡️You can take your time walking to class and enjoy the campus.

slow down your step 是中文「放慢腳步」直譯，英文通常說 take your time。

08But, if you are worried about the quality in school dorm. You may properably choose to live outside.FRAGSPWW
➡️However, if you care more about privacy, you may prefer to live off campus.

if 子句不能單獨成句，要用逗號接主要子句。probably 拼錯。But 不放句首加逗號，用 However。另外，這句在幫另一個選項講話，削弱了自己的立場，建議刪掉。

09I would more prefer to live in the school dorm.WW
➡️I would prefer to live in the school dorm.

prefer 本身就有「比較喜歡」的意思，不能再加 more。

PARAGRAPH 2

10living in school dormART
➡️living in a school dorm

dorm 是可數名詞，單數要加冠詞。

11might be a wonderful experience instead of facing a lot of obstaclesLOGCONT
➡️might be a wonderful experience, even though it may bring some challenges

這句和題目衝突。題目要他討論會遇到的挑戰，這句卻說「不會遇到障礙」，等於否定了題目。改成「雖然會有挑戰」才接得上後面的內容。

12Although I haven't graduated from seniorMISS
➡️Although I am still a high school student

senior 後面少了 high school。直接說「我還是高中生」最簡單。

13I have an experience to live in school dorm during my summer campWWT
➡️I once stayed in a school dorm during a summer camp

have an experience to 是中式說法。這是過去的事，用過去式 stayed，加上 once 表示「曾經」。

14it gave me a shocked that I have never seen beforeWWT
➡️I was shocked by what I saw

shocked 是形容詞，不能當 give 的受詞。主詞應該是「我」：I was shocked。

15Four beds and tables are in a small room.T
➡️Four beds and four desks were squeezed into a tiny room.

整段在講過去的經驗，要用過去式。squeezed into 可以畫出「很擠」的感覺。

16The bed was very thick that makes me can't sleep very well.WWCHT
➡️The bed was so hard that I could not sleep well.

「太…以致於」是 so…that，不是 very…that。make me can't 不合文法（make + 受詞 + 原形動詞），直接用 I could not 最清楚。thick 是「厚」，床很厚不會睡不好，應該是想說 hard（硬）。

17The hardest thing I think is wash my clothes.WWT
➡️The hardest part, I think, was doing laundry.

be 動詞後面接動作要用 V-ing 或 to V。I think 插在句中要用逗號隔開。洗衣服可以說 do laundry。

18I need to consider what time and which days to wash my clothes.T
➡️Because the washing machines were shared, I had to plan when to do my laundry.

改成過去式。補上「洗衣機是共用的」，讀者才知道為什麼要排時間。這是很好的細節，值得保留。

19it can makes me more grow and can face the problemsSVWW
➡️it can help me grow and learn to deal with problems

can 後面接原形動詞，不能加 s。more grow 不合文法，grow 本身就是「成長」。

20just like a fish in a waterWWART
➡️just like a duck to water

water 不可數，不加 a。英文沒有 like a fish in water 這個說法；常見的 like a fish out of water 意思是「格格不入」，剛好相反。「很快就適應」要說 take to it like a duck to water。

05

高分優化建議

切題度：第二段一定要寫解決方法

  • 這是最大的扣分點。挑戰已經想好兩個，各補一句解法就能把內容分拉上來：
  • 床太硬：自己帶床墊或睡袋鋪在上面。
  • 洗衣服：和室友排好洗衣時間表，或趁人少的早上去洗。
  • 空間太小：用收納箱整理東西，和室友約好作息規則。
  • 寫法：每個挑戰後面緊接著寫解法，不要先把挑戰全部列完，最後才用一句話帶過。

組織邏輯：立場放在第一句

  • 到第一段最後一句才說要住宿舍，閱卷老師讀了一整段才知道選哪個。第一句就要說：If I were given the choice, I would definitely live in the school dorm.
  • 刪掉 But, if you are worried about…：題目要選一邊並說明原因，替另一邊講話會讓立場模糊。
  • 三個理由可以精簡成兩個講清楚。「安全」目前邏輯不通，可以刪掉，或改寫成有室友照應、有宿舍管理員。

06

滿分示範改寫

保留原本的想法：比較便宜、離教室近、夏令營住宿經驗、四人擠一間、床太硬、洗衣服要排時間、讓自己成長。新增的只有三句解決方法和「學會和人相處」這個收穫。

After graduating from high school, many students have to choose between living in a school dorm and renting an apartment. If I were given the choice, I would definitely live in the school dorm. To begin with, living in a dorm is much cheaper than renting an apartment, so I could save money for entertainment and transportation, or even for my future. Moreover, a dorm is usually located on campus, just a few minutes away from the classrooms. Instead of rushing through traffic every morning, I could take my time walking to class and enjoy the beauty of the campus. For these reasons, the school dorm is clearly the better choice for me.

Of course, living in a dorm comes with its own challenges. Although I am still a high school student, I once stayed in a school dorm during a summer camp, and I was shocked by what I saw. Four beds and four desks were squeezed into a tiny room, and the bed was so hard that I could not sleep well. To solve this problem, I would bring a soft mattress pad to make my bed more comfortable. The hardest part, however, was doing laundry. Because the washing machines were shared by everyone on the floor, I had to plan carefully when to wash my clothes. In college, I would make a laundry schedule with my roommates so that we would not have to wait in line. Overcoming these challenges would not only make me more independent but also teach me how to get along with others. In the end, I believe I would take to dorm life like a duck to water.
